# Replacement firmware packages

Owned X-M1 replacement code is built through the existing Bazel/Nix graph. Each package stays separate so the shared build declarations change in one place.

- [`toolchain/`](toolchain/README.md): the pinned `armv7r-none-eabi` rustc, its `core` and `compiler_builtins`, and the `xm1_rust_staticlib` / `xm1_rust_elf` macros.
- [`abi/`](abi/README.md): the #119 build and ABI qualification, including the bounded native case, the ABI defect control and the facts #120/#123 consume.
- `first_stage/`: the owned first-record startup staged at slot offset 0x6000 by `//host/firmware:build-first-stage`, with the stock record-1 helpers retained. The correct candidate's natural-reset handoff witness passes on the public runtime pin (E-FIRST-STAGE-PUBLIC-HANDOFF-150) and on combined SDK `97a6ebe` (E-FIRST-STAGE-COMBINED-SDK-HANDOFF-150). On that combined SDK the faulty-handoff and mismatched-image controls fail at their intended checks and the staged DAT cold-boots to SHOOTING MENU (1, 5, 2) (E-FIRST-STAGE-COMBINED-SDK-MENU-CONTROLS-150). The earlier private YIELD-corrected runtime results are E-FIRST-STAGE-NATURAL-HANDOFF-150 and E-FIRST-STAGE-MENU-CONTROLS-150. Full replacement startup, runtime retirement and hardware startup remain separate.
- `secondary_stage/`: the owned record-2 startup for [#135](https://github.com/skeet70/latent-image/issues/135), staged at record-2 slot offset `0x400` (guest `0x10400`) by `//host/firmware:build-secondary-stage`. Rust owns the entry decisions, MPU programming, the companion startup sequence of stock `0x11cc0` including its peripheral predicates, the event log, the rendezvous and the loader-B publication, which keeps stock's unchecked segment dereference; `entry.S` calls the retained execution-state and TCM/stack services and performs the SVC `mov pc,r3` handoff. `adapter.rs` holds the recovered addresses and the typed retained-service ABI, and `secondary-stage.ld` the eleven service entries, the retained vector/body/data extents and the candidate forbidden fetch/read ranges. The [startup inventory](../reports/replacement-inventory-baseline.md#owned-record-2-source-increment-135) records the service ABI and the loader-B register evidence. The schema-3 cohort accepts all five retained and five owned scenarios, four endpoint comparisons and the intended publication/stack fault refusals (`E-SECONDARY-STAGE-SCHEMA3-SCENARIOS-150`). The retired-stock-fetch control is refused at core1 fetch of `0x12960`, before startup handoff or loader-B entry, and the public schema3 consumer refuses its receipt (`E-SECONDARY-RETIRED-FETCH-CONTROL-150`). This does not qualify full startup or hardware.
- `loader_prefix/`: [#136](https://github.com/skeet70/latent-image/issues/136) compiles the secondary startup and loader-B core-1 prefix into one record-2 image. The staged loader-B entry names the interior prefix symbol. Since the #155 J2 re-cut, Rust owns vector installation and assembly owns privileged setup, the eight-word `stm` workspace clear and the exit `b 0xd09ac` with `lr=0xd00c0` on the stock stack; stock loader B performs the core decision and the completion wait, and core1 reaches retained initialization `0xd0aac` through it. Core0 continues through retained `0xd09d0`. The following results describe the pre-J2 prefix, which owned the decision and the wait: `E-LOADER-B-PREFIX-POSITIVE-150` accepts eight standalone retained/owned natural, held, delayed and non-one guests and four independently consumed comparisons with zero differences. Natural completion orders the original store and committed readback before core1 exit; held completion observes four post-gate zero loads without a store or endpoint; delayed completion preserves the original store and veneer context; non-one completion commits/readbacks `0x400a0000`. Frame, vectors, physical/virtual views and all 22,112 zero-store words agree. The q5 pre-prefix failure remains history (`E-LOADER-B-PREFIX-INITIAL-REFUSAL-150`), not the current verdict. Seven discriminating prefix fault guests are refused at the intended write-confinement, retired-fetch, exit-vector, saved-frame, core-refusal, fill and stack boundaries. Explicit injected-invalid-core and zero-accepting-predicate scenarios executed and produced negative evidence; J2 removed both scenarios and the predicate fault image.
- `interface_initializer/`: a distinct successor to the core1 prefix, covering the complete `0xd0430` interface/descriptor initializer through preexecution `0xd0ab0`. It repeats all 1419 volatile publications in Rust and uses a narrow A32 exact-return adapter. `//emulation:build-interface-initializer` binds the DAT, staged image, symbols and object-derived compiler scratch extent; schema6 has typed natural retained/owned SDK and independent Haskell consumers. Explicit opt-in schema7 wraps the complete schema6 evidence and observes the retained interrupt-mask service through caller preexecution `0x72360c`. Natural retained and owned schema6/7 runs and their comparisons are accepted, and single-field receipt mutants are refused (`E-STARTUP-NATURAL-V7-150`). Original core0 initialization, all installed service targets/pools and the later kernel remain retained. See the [initializer source contract](../emulation/README.md#core-1-interface-initializer-witness-schema-6) and [retained consumer source contract](../emulation/README.md#retained-interrupt-mask-consumer-witness-schema-7).
- `primary_initializer/`: complete primary publication for [#144](https://github.com/skeet70/latent-image/issues/144), bounded in place to `[0x40430,0x409ac)`. Its independent Rust publisher uses A-specific services, tick, extensions and 120 descriptors; the private A32 adapter preserves the caller ABI. The qualified linked carrier is 1404 bytes, with a 708-byte payload and 32 total derived scratch bytes; pure DAT staging preserves every outside byte except the rebuilt main checksum. Natural retained and owned schema8 producers and schema9 root0 consumption on the natural ATCM IRQ stack are accepted with matching retained/owned semantics. - Composed startup: [#137](https://github.com/skeet70/latent-image/issues/137) uses `//host/firmware:build-composed-startup` to stage the owned first stage in record1 and the single linked `loader_prefix` image in record2, not an overlay of standalone images. `E-COMPOSED-STARTUP-HOST-SDK-150` source-qualifies the aggregate DAT builder and schema5 observer, including four actual composed natural/held/delayed/non-one DATs and independent byte-preservation/checksum/identity proofs. With the continuous seven-store handler protocol and checked completion counters, a natural composed schema5 receipt and a retained schema4 receipt are accepted and their public comparison passes (`E-COMPOSED-NATURAL-POSITIVE-150`). Held, delayed and non-one composed scenarios and their matching retained runs also pass launch-time and independent consumption, with all three comparisons accepted (`E-COMPOSED-COMPLETION-SCENARIOS-150`). The wrong-handoff, wrong-first-stage-image, preinstallation handler-tail-write and explicit interleaved-tail controls are refused.
- `task_storage/`: #143 reservation/reuse transition inside retained helper `0x725c6c`. Its unchanged native source actually links exactly `[0x725cc4,0x725da8)`: a six-instruction, 24-byte adapter and 204-byte Rust body. - `startup_dispatch/`: [#146](https://github.com/skeet70/latent-image/issues/146) finite image4 startup phase dispatcher, in place at `[0x69cdf8,0x69cf84)`. - `plain_load/`: [#153](https://github.com/skeet70/latent-image/issues/153) complete plain-image orchestration at `[0x6a9844,0x6a9938)`. Freestanding Rust preserves both mode branches, temporal reads, missing-segment halt, ignored resource statuses and fresh unconditional final-length progress. Host admission/staging and one natural retained/owned read-success pair qualify the finite producer (`E-LATER-PLAIN-PRODUCER-HOST-150`, `E-LATER-PLAIN-PRODUCER-NATURAL-150`). Derived caller scratch reaches SP-40. ## General heap production staging

`//replacement/general_heap:general-heap-image` builds the original arena variant; `general-heap-recipe-image` selects ceiling `0xbe1a00` instead of `0xbf1a00`. Both charge state to the arena prefix and keep the 32-byte guard. The linker fails on carrier overflow without selecting a relocated fallback. `//replacement/general_heap:general-heap-tests` contains host-only source/artifact/header/geometry/fetch controls. `heap-bridge-replay` consumes the admitted lifetime tape through the production stock bridge.

`//replacement/general_heap:general-heap-host-tests` and `general-heap-recipe-host-tests` build the pinned Nix host-test derivations for the original and recipe ceilings. Each runs the behavioral Rust oracle inside the derivation and exports its Cargo lockfile only after success; these are Bazel build targets, not separate Cargo test commands.

`//replacement/general_heap:stage-general-heap` takes `SOURCE_DAT IMAGE4 IMAGE5 LINKED_BIN SYMBOLS SCRATCH_DIR OUT_IMAGE4 OUT_IMAGE5 OUT_DAT OUT_CONTRACT` after its Bazel-supplied encoder/rebuilder/decoder arguments. Inputs must match canonical X-M1 1.50 identities. The scratch directory must exist below `private-data/scratch`. | --- | --- | --- |
| Base nonzero, saved requested size differs | `-33` | No transition writes |
| Base nonzero, matching size, reserved nonzero | `-18` | No transition writes |
| Base nonzero, matching size, reserved zero | `0` | Store the previously loaded base to output; store record reserved `1` |

| Base zero, capacity admitted | `0` | Store `cursor & ~0x40000000` to output; reload cursor; add `requested & ~3` with wrapping arithmetic; store cursor; reload output; store record base, original requested size and reserved `1`, in that order |

Capacity admission uses the unmasked request and unsigned modular arithmetic.

The task103 constructor at `0x69cef8..0x69cf34`, reached for stage4 input `4`, writes descriptor words `{103,0x781970,15,0x400,0x69cbf0,0}`. It does not initialize descriptor word `+0x18`. Class-table word `0x7caf34` is `2`. Absent intervening changes, the helper call has index `102`, request `0x400`, output/wrapper base `0x40a5b534`, wrapper size `0x40a5b538` and state `0x40a1010c`. The helper path remains conditional on the two wrapper reads. Direct helper execution cannot establish that natural path.

Caller `0x72623c` invokes that release after a retained service `+4` call even when that service returns an error. Subsequent reuse can observe the saved pair and cleared reservation. ## Owned record store

### Core crate

`replacement/record_store` implements the allocation-free `no_std` core and nine stock-ABI guest entries from [the owned record-store design](../reports/owned-record-store-design.md) over the retained raw NAND service. [The stock contract](../reports/record-service-stock-contract.md) supplies the search, callback, rotation, staging, collection and output-write expectations. `RecordMedium`, `Staging` and `Callbacks` separate the core from guest addresses. `Service` owns five registrations; `Entry` holds one caller-provided 128 KiB block buffer for one operation, invalidated after every program or erase. Program failure is an `Appended.persisted` value; an erase failure leaving a full destination produces `NotAppended::DestinationFull`. The adapter preserves stock's swallowed program/erase statuses.

Build `//replacement/record_store:record-store-rust` for the ARMv7-R guest static archive (`cortex-r4f`, opt-level `z`, panic abort, one codegen unit). `record-store-rust-control-1` through `-9` each build the same guest adapter and core with one design §5 fault. Carrier linking and DAT staging are separate targets owned by the placement increment.

Every public guest archive is gated by `:archive-check`: its owned crate's undefined symbols must contain neither an AEABI name nor a symbol defined by a bundled compiler-builtins member. The archive still bundles unused sysroot members; the carrier separately rejects reachable compiler-builtins sections at final link. Variable division uses an owned shift/subtract operation, wrapper lookup does not divide pointer differences, and guest initialization writes cell activity flags in place. The guest's owned fill loop and `no_builtins` attribute avoid memory-helper calls.

`//replacement/record_store:host-tests` compiles and executes the core's behavioural host suite. `:host-adapter-tests` also exercises all nine exported entries, client-word nulling and preservation, fixed staging pointers, statuses and shared-buffer lifetime across reentry. Tag selection, fill and staging copy hold semaphore `0x4b`; callbacks and persistence calls run outside it. The host medium erases to `0xff` and programs by bitwise AND. `:host-control-1` through `-9` each compile the core suite with one control feature and succeed only when that executable fails. Build through the checked-in environment and Bazel.

`:host-adapter-control-6` runs only the guest ABI test under `guest` plus `control-6` and requires a failure. The fixture fills bank zero to N records, appends, and reads back the medium through `record_store_read_at`: stock leaves the bytes at bank-zero offset N*S erased and rotates to bank one; control 6 programs at N*S without rotation. Control 6 changes inner append's refusal threshold from N-1 to N, not read latest's recovery-probe equality.

The core has no fixed addresses. The guest imports the retained raw region-read, per-kind write/erase and semaphore entries. Its linker-defined storage holds initialization, wrappers, registration state, two cache buffers and their metadata, and per-kind staging. Compile-time sizing bounds the owned registration state to the retired slots' `0x168` bytes.

The host cases cover the five stock geometries and defined registration/refusal paths. RSC's undefined zero-size division, malformed kinds entering helpers and invalid caller pointers are not promoted into a safe total guest contract. Host source tests and must-fail controls do not establish stock-byte equivalence, physical NAND laws or a full-system cutover.
### Placement

The nine stock entries occupy `[0x6ac2b4,0x6ae114)`. Minimal ARM branch veneers consume four bytes each; the remaining runs are 112, 332, 76, 776, 428, 304, 740, 4,952 and 20 bytes, totaling 7,740. Eight-byte veneers leave 7,704 bytes. The design's final 24-byte run includes the last entry and is not wholly free.

The split carrier uses four-byte entry branches and linker assertions for every occupied run and veneer address. The flat default and control images retain the 7,776-byte stock extent, including gaps, and end at `0x6ae114`. Reachable compiler-builtins sections are forbidden. ## Own-first RTOS slot bodies (#156)

`replacement/rtos_layout` (crate `xm1_rtos_layout`, `no_std`) holds the stock RTOS data layout of both kernels, shared by `rtos_own_first` and `rtos_scheduler`: `Domain::words()` gives each loader's `DomainWords` (context word, scheduler and object lock words, current-context pointer, the two context records, g_reschedule, ready heads and bitmap, running, dispatch-enabled and selected cells, clock words, timer heap and T table), the views `KernelContext` (0x84 bytes), `LockRecord`, `ReadyHead`, `Task` (0x38 bytes) and `TaskDefinition` (+0x0c priority word, +0x10/+0x14 initial-SP terms, from `kernel_reset_task` and `FUN_00044058`) give field addresses, and `DomainId`, `TaskSlot` (1..=120), `Priority` (0..=15), `TaskState` (T+0x0c, bits 0x3f) and `TaskFlags` (T+0x0e) are the typed values. Users include `src/lib.rs` as a `#[path]` module; `:rtos-layout-host-tests` pins every address to the stock literals.

`replacement/rtos_own_first` (crate `xm1_rtos_own_first`) owns the 17 own-first root slots of both loaders in place. `:own-first-a` and `:own-first-b` link through `own-first-a.ld` / `own-first-b.ld` with `--gc-sections --orphan-handling=error`: one MEMORY region per stock extent, each entry asserted at its region start and each body inside its extent, any `.rodata`, `.data` or `.bss` refused, and every unused byte filled with UDF `0xe7f000f0` by the linker. Staging writes the changed words through `F.applyLoaderWordPatches`, `U.rebuildContainer` and `F.verifyPreTablePatches`, refuses any DAT byte change outside the extents and the main checksum at `0x20c`, checks the candidate's loaders against the staged images, and requires `restore` of the candidate to give the canonical DAT. `bazel run //replacement/rtos_own_first/stage:stage-own-first -- SOURCE_DAT LOADER_A LOADER_B NEW_OUTPUT_DIRECTORY` checks the pinned 1.50 inputs and writes `candidate.DAT`, `loader-a.bin`, `loader-b.bin` and `contract.json` (per loader and slot: stock and owned extent sha256, entry symbol, frame demand with its calls, the tail body placement, and the stock-entry-to-owned-symbol table). `:own-first-stage-tests` stages a two-slot region set on the synthetic container of `//replacement/startup_artifact:Fixtures.hs` and checks one refusal per reachable admission and staging check.

`replacement/rtos_own_first/oracle` is the native-case oracle (`Replacement.RtosOwnFirst.Oracle`, `.Controls`, `.Verdict`, `.Assembly`). Per loader and slot it runs one case grid against the stock body from the pinned loader (the stock extent, plus the helper `0x427b8`/`0xd27b8` for +0x1618 and the +0x148 and +0x128 extents for +0x16c) and against the owned body (its installed region, the callee regions for +0x16c and the host region of the +0x1618 body), all read and execute. The grid crosses SVC/SYS, entry I and F and three NZCVQ/GE patterns with each slot's dimensions from the design (context and dispatch words, current T, the T+0xe byte, +0x168's r0, +0x1618's CP14 c2 value) and adds 64 fixed-seed xorshift rows per slot. A pair matches when status, r0, r4-r11, sp, CPSR mode/T/F/I/A, and the final bytes and ordered write log of the data regions and the caller frame agree; flags, lr and the stack below entry sp are not compared. The stock body must return on every case. The full oracle, `bazel run //replacement/rtos_own_first/oracle:own-first-oracle -- --loader-a LOADER_A --loader-b LOADER_B --work NEW_DIRECTORY --summary NEW_JSON` with the private `loader-40000.bin` and `loader-d0000.bin`, writes the summary (per loader and slot: status counts of the stock and owned runs, cases, matches and the first mismatch; per control: status counts, differing cases that returned and that stopped, and whether it holds; input sha256s) and keeps batch directories only for a slot that fails; it exits 1 when a stock run stops or a control misbehaves, and 2 when an owned body differs from stock. The structured rows also run every slot on the modeled IRQ-stack address range `[0x70000000,0x70002000)`, with SP `0x70001ff0`, for CP14 c2 identities `0x8003` and `0x4003`. This is flat native-case memory, not an emulation of private TCM or two concurrent cores. The native runner admits SVC and SYS, not IRQ mode; both modes include I-set entries. The +0x164 grid additionally runs original caller stubs for `save -> restore -> save` and an outer save containing an inner save/restore followed by the outer restore. Each call records r0 and the complete CPSR in `call-trace`, whose bytes and ordered writes are compared. Failing slots retain `differences-LOADER-SLOT.json` with every differing row, input context, compared registers, memory bytes and writes for stock and owned, alongside the native batch artifacts.

Add `--timing-only` to run just the structured single-call rows of +0x128, +0x148, +0x164, +0x168, +0x16c and +0x1618, without repeat/equivalent/mutant controls. This mode retains its raw native reports and writes `timing-LOADER-SLOT.json` with each input row and stock/owned instruction counts. Counts include the helper/callees and the return instruction but exclude the exit stub. Conditional instructions that fail their condition still count as entered instructions. These are native-runner instruction costs, not hardware cycles.

Add `--generate COUNT --seed SEED` to the same oracle to compare a deterministic valid +0x16c corpus against stock, the owned body and `mutant-s16c` in both loaders. Counts are 1..256 per loader. Current-task words are record starts in the domain's 120-record table; entries use SVC or SYS, and context words are zero or nonzero. Null, misaligned and negative task offsets are outside this generated domain. The unsigned 64-bit decimal or hexadecimal seed folds to a 32-bit xorshift seed; distinct seeds can collide.

Use the worker's normal `BAZEL_OUTPUT_BASE` and canonical X-M1 1.50 extracted loaders. The Bazel graph supplies the linked candidates, regions and pinned native runner. Each work directory and summary must be new:

Reduction preserves validity and the original typed comparison boundary, such as `RegisterKind R0`, while lowering task record, entry flags, F, mode and context form. `--shrink-budget` bounds trials to 1..256, default 32. Flags are cleared together rather than searched by subset. `fixed-point` is local to these reductions; `budget-exhausted` establishes no minimum. The summary names original and reduced rows, differences, identities and native batch paths; the reduced case is re-executed through the same comparator.

`--replay` re-executes both saved cases for each recorded discrepancy. All pairs' runner, loader, linked-input, case and wire identities are admitted before the first execution. Paired cases must agree on non-code state and metadata. Replay copies only each selected case and its declared artifacts, verifies the copied identities and leaves the preserved reports unchanged. Typed nonempty boundary kinds decide reproduction; diagnostic wording does not. Work and summary paths are canonical across the `private-data` symlink.

Every oracle mode uses the existing emulator slot with the 2 GiB cap and unchanged 100 GiB reserve. Completed generate and replay attempts print a compact terminal result and summary path. Generate exits 1 for a stock stop or missing returned control witness, 2 for an owned difference, and 0 for scoped agreement with the control refused. Replay exits 1 for an incomplete execution or changed boundary, 2 for a reproduced owned discrepancy, and 0 for reproduced controls. Input or admission refusals stop before execution. This corpus is additional native coverage, not full subsystem, firmware or hardware acceptance.

### Owned scheduler helpers, step S1 (#156)

`replacement/rtos_scheduler` (crate `xm1_rtos_scheduler`, `no_std`, no statics) owns the nine SCHED helpers of both loaders behind their stock entries: lock acquire and release (A `0x439d8`/`0x43a14`, B `0xd398c`/`0xd39c8`), find (`0x43c48`/`0xd3c04`), insert (`0x43ca4`/`0xd3c60`), remove (`0x43d20`/`0xd3cdc`), reset (`0x43dd0`/`0xd3d8c`), prepare (`0x43f70`/`0xd3f2c`), notify (`0x44018`/`0xd3fd4`), requeue (`0x44044`/`0xd4004`) and rotate (`0x44134`/`0xd40f4`). Each helper is one `Machine`-generic function in `src/lib.rs` over the `rtos_layout` views (`KernelContext`, `LockRecord`, `ReadyHead`, `Task`, `TaskDefinition`) and `src/entries.rs` runs it as a `#[no_mangle] extern "C"` A32 entry in `.text.<helper>`, built per domain by `--cfg feature="domain-a"` or `"domain-b"`. Every helper keeps the stock A32 register ABI: arguments, the r0 result (release returns its argument, reset its task, requeue `*dispatch_enabled != 0` in its branch for the selected task and the raw word otherwise), callee-saved registers and sp, and the T-field, list, bitmap and selected-cell stores in stock order with stock values. The lock pair is `ldrex`/`cmp`/`strexeq` then `dmb sy`, and `dmb sy`, store 0, `dsb sy`; prepare issues `dmb sy` between its T+0x18/+0x1c stores and its tail call into insert; notify keeps each loader's local-id predicate (A `id <= 1`, B `id == 0 || id == 2`), and for a remote id issues `dsb sy` and calls the stock raise-IRQ function (`0x433e4`/`0xd33e4`) with `id asr 1` and 120. Priority bytes and arguments are taken unmasked as stock does, under the invariant that they are below 16; find masks its trailing-zero count to four bits, so an empty bitmap selects list 0 where stock reads a byte before its table.

`:scheduler-a` and `:scheduler-b` link through `scheduler-a.ld` / `scheduler-b.ld` with `--gc-sections --orphan-handling=error`: one MEMORY region per stock helper extent at its stock origin, each entry asserted at its region start and each body inside its extent, `.rodata`/`.data`/`.bss` refused, unused bytes UDF `0xe7f000f0`, and the raise-IRQ entry an absolute symbol. Target `armv7r-none-eabi` forces frame pointers, so prepare's body (48 bytes) does not fit its 44-byte extent: the prepare entry is a `b` to `xm1_rtos_scheduler_prepare_body` in the last 48 bytes of the find extent (A `0x43c74`, B `0xd3c30`), a shim to remove when the stock layout no longer constrains the extents. `regions.json` lists per domain the ten regions with owned and stock frame demand in bytes composed through callees, the raise-IRQ address and the prepare shim; `:scheduler-regions-tests` checks it against both links' symbols and checks every `frame_bytes` against a walk of the linked disassembly (first push, plus the deepest `bl` callee, or the frame of a tail `b` target; stock raise IRQ counts 4). `:scheduler-host-tests` runs the helpers on a model machine (queue contents, selected cell, results, store order, barrier and doorbell order), and `:scheduler-bin-digests-tests` pins both `.bin`s.

Peak stack below entry, owned against stock, in bytes (both loaders): acquire and release 8 / 4, find 8 / 4, insert 16 / 8, remove 24 / 20, reset 8 / 4, prepare 16 / 16, notify 12 / 12 (local path 0 / 8), requeue 32 / 28, rotate 8 / 8. A retained caller grows by at most 8 bytes (through insert), and every locking function by 4 through the lock pair. No test checks these against the task, IRQ or null-loop stacks.

Known differences from stock that no caller observes: NZCV, lr, r1-r3 and r12 at return; requeue reads the selected cell through the pointer it already holds instead of reloading record+0x2c (the reload does not fit the 240-byte extent); find computes the lowest set bit with `rbit`/`clz` instead of reading stock's nibble table (A `0x4daa0`, B `0xdda64`), which gives the same list for every bitmap with a bit in 0..15.

`rtos_scheduler/oracle` is the S1 native-case oracle. The grid covers both loaders, local and remote records, priorities 0, 5 and 15, empty, single and three-task lists with the task at head, middle, tail or alone, selected cells null, more urgent, less urgent, equal and the task itself, consistent and stale bitmaps, dispatch-enabled 0, 1, 2 and `0x80000000`, every reachable T+0x0c state, T+0x0e flag bytes, notify ids 0, 1, 2, 3, `0xffffffff` and `0x80000000`, and free and held words of both lock records.
