# Replacement firmware packages

Owned X-M1 replacement code is built through the existing Bazel/Nix graph. Each package stays separate so the shared build declarations change in one place.

- [`toolchain/`](toolchain/README.md): the pinned `armv7r-none-eabi` rustc, its `core` and `compiler_builtins`, and the `xm1_rust_staticlib` / `xm1_rust_elf` macros.
- [`abi/`](abi/README.md): the #119 build and ABI qualification, including the bounded native case, the ABI defect control and the facts #120/#123 consume.
- `first_stage/`: the owned first-record startup staged at slot offset 0x6000 by `//host/firmware:build-first-stage`, with the stock record-1 helpers retained. The correct candidate's natural-reset handoff witness passes on the public runtime pin (E-FIRST-STAGE-PUBLIC-HANDOFF-150) and on combined SDK `97a6ebe` (E-FIRST-STAGE-COMBINED-SDK-HANDOFF-150). On that combined SDK the faulty-handoff and mismatched-image controls fail at their intended checks and the staged DAT cold-boots to SHOOTING MENU (1, 5, 2) (E-FIRST-STAGE-COMBINED-SDK-MENU-CONTROLS-150). The earlier private YIELD-corrected runtime results are E-FIRST-STAGE-NATURAL-HANDOFF-150 and E-FIRST-STAGE-MENU-CONTROLS-150. Full replacement startup, runtime retirement and hardware startup remain separate.
- `secondary_stage/`: the owned record-2 startup for [#135](https://github.com/skeet70/latent-image/issues/135), staged at record-2 slot offset `0x400` (guest `0x10400`) by `//host/firmware:build-secondary-stage`. Rust owns the entry decisions, MPU programming, the companion startup sequence of stock `0x11cc0` including its peripheral predicates, the event log, the rendezvous and the loader-B publication, which keeps stock's unchecked segment dereference; `entry.S` calls the retained execution-state and TCM/stack services and performs the SVC `mov pc,r3` handoff. `adapter.rs` holds the recovered addresses and the typed retained-service ABI, and `secondary-stage.ld` the eleven service entries, the retained vector/body/data extents and the candidate forbidden fetch/read ranges. The [startup inventory](../reports/replacement-inventory-baseline.md#owned-record-2-source-increment-135) records the service ABI and the loader-B register evidence. The schema-3 cohort accepts all five retained and five owned scenarios, four endpoint comparisons and the intended publication/stack fault refusals (`E-SECONDARY-STAGE-SCHEMA3-SCENARIOS-150`). An actual forbidden-stock-fetch fault control remains open; this does not qualify full startup or hardware.
- `loader_prefix/`: [#136](https://github.com/skeet70/latent-image/issues/136) compiles the secondary startup and loader-B core-1 prefix into one record-2 image. Rust owns vector installation, workspace clearing and the unbounded nonzero completion wait; assembly handles privileged setup and the caller frame. The staged loader-B entry names the interior prefix symbol. Core0 continues through retained `0xd09d0`; core1 exits at retained initialization `0xd0aac`. `E-LOADER-B-PREFIX-POSITIVE-150` accepts eight standalone retained/owned natural, held, delayed and non-one guests and four independently consumed comparisons with zero differences. Natural completion orders the original store and committed readback before core1 exit; held completion observes four post-gate zero loads without a store or endpoint; delayed completion preserves the original store and veneer context; non-one completion commits/readbacks `0x400a0000`. Frame, vectors, physical/virtual views and all 22,112 zero-store words agree. The q5 pre-prefix failure remains history (`E-LOADER-B-PREFIX-INITIAL-REFUSAL-150`), not the current verdict. Discriminating fault controls remain unaccepted and authentication-held.
- `interface_initializer/`: a distinct source-only successor to the core1 prefix, covering the complete `0xd0430` interface/descriptor initializer through preexecution `0xd0ab0`. It repeats all 1419 volatile publications in Rust and uses a narrow A32 exact-return adapter. `//emulation:build-interface-initializer` binds the DAT, staged image, symbols and object-derived compiler scratch extent; schema6 has typed natural retained/owned SDK and independent Haskell consumers. Explicit opt-in schema7 wraps the complete schema6 evidence and observes the retained interrupt-mask service through caller preexecution `0x72360c`, with source-bound code, actual frames, ordered effects and a retained/owned comparison. Original core0 initialization, all installed service targets/pools and the later kernel remain retained. No new build or runtime acceptance is claimed. See the [initializer source contract](../emulation/README.md#core-1-interface-initializer-witness-schema-6) and [retained consumer source contract](../emulation/README.md#retained-interrupt-mask-consumer-witness-schema-7).
- `primary_initializer/`: source-only complete primary publication for [#144](https://github.com/skeet70/latent-image/issues/144), bounded in place to `[0x40430,0x409ac)`. Its independent Rust publisher uses A-specific services, tick, extensions and 120 descriptors; the private A32 adapter preserves the caller ABI. `//emulation:build-primary-initializer` admits the actual linked bytes and symbols, derives compiler scratch and complete ordered writes, and stages only the entire owned window plus the DAT main checksum. Source contracts bind root-0 installation, selection and the bounded descriptor access without claiming arrival or whole-leaf retirement. Fit, emitted scratch and natural owned producer/consumer qualification remain unobserved; existing schema8 remains retained-only.
- Composed startup: [#137](https://github.com/skeet70/latent-image/issues/137) uses `//host/firmware:build-composed-startup` to stage the owned first stage in record1 and the single linked `loader_prefix` image in record2, not an overlay of standalone images. `E-COMPOSED-STARTUP-HOST-SDK-150` source-qualifies the aggregate DAT builder and schema5 observer, including four actual composed natural/held/delayed/non-one DATs and independent byte-preservation/checksum/identity proofs. Current linked extents are record1 `[0x6000,0x64ac)` and record2 `[0x10400,0x10a6c)`, with prefix entry `0x1042c`. Historical host checks and scoped SDK admission pass; the quantum1 CLI smoke intentionally refuses scheduling with zero guest instructions. The first runtime attempt stopped at owned record1 entry on identity-span admission. Fix `8c8ad50` admits adjacent readable mappings with continuous physical translation while refusing gaps, overlaps and discontinuities (`E-SDK-CONTIGUOUS-SPAN-150`). `E-COMPOSED-HANDOFF-ORDER-REFUSAL-150` preserves the next composed-natural attempt: secondary entry and record2 handoff are observed, then the observer refuses because core0 first-stage handoff has not yet occurred. The full initial record2 identity is observed; live-projection/scoped binding, first-stage handoff and prefix/completion/endpoint are not. The other eleven guests and all comparisons were not started. The integrated partial-order observer retains the mandatory first-stage handoff through core0's later continuation boundary. `E-COMPOSED-TEMPORAL-SOURCE-150` separately qualifies isolated SDK `94ceeac`: 96 default, 103 scoped and 96 readback checks, 104 distinct logical-target-qualified names, including two scoped tests covering synthetic Early/Late acceptance and Missing/Invalid refusal. Earlier Graph-mapping and colliding-entry/load fixture failures remain history. The fresh SDK/workspace matrix and six pre-machine usage refusals are source/package qualification, not later diagnostic/initializer qualification or composed firmware execution. No composed positive or comparison is accepted; fresh cohort preparation does not imply compilation or guest execution. Standalone prefix positives do not qualify this composition, and composed fault controls remain authentication-held.
- `task_storage/`: #143 reservation/reuse transition inside retained helper `0x725c6c`. Its unchanged native source actually links exactly `[0x725cc4,0x725da8)`: a six-instruction, 24-byte adapter and 204-byte Rust body. The original synchronization prelude/epilogue and conditional wrapper remain retained. Public artifact admission now carries a source-only conditional emitted-frame/AAPCS and symbolic memory-footprint proof. `//replacement/task_storage:stage-task-storage` uses shared `Emulation.Image4Staging` and the existing encoder/repacker. Host admission, staging, natural caller, alias/scratch separation and lifetime closure remain unqualified.

The first-stage witness checks the guest handoff target against loader A's entry word and the staged owned bytes against the supplied candidate. A producer receipt with `result: passed` records completed observation, not consumer acceptance. The negative controls reach the handoff and collect all twelve reads without a model failure: the faulty candidate exposes target `0xdeadbe00` instead of `0x40020`, while the mismatched-image control runs the correct DAT against the faulty candidate's expected image digest. Both return nonzero from the witness consumer. The correct DAT also boots from natural reset through the fresh-settings English/date route to a rendered SHOOTING MENU page 1; visual acceptance remains the owner's check.

Record 2's retained services, vectors and data, the loaders, application images and retained record-1 helpers remain proprietary execution dependencies. These results do not establish application/runtime retirement, full replacement startup, hardware behavior, silicon timing or persistence.

The corrected standalone schema4 and composed schema5 observers require both the full initial record2 identity at preexecution secondary entry and the mandatory later immutable projection. Only `[0x14c60,0x14c6c)` is excluded from that projection; the final twenty bytes through `0x14c80` remain protected. Accepted standalone guests exercise both observations on the current scoped SDK. Continuous immutable-byte, forbidden-fetch and core1 write-confinement checks cover only the declared record2-handoff-to-final-stop interval, armed at handoff before prefix entry, not all startup or runtime. The composed loaded guard protects the owned first-stage image/vectors and stock entry instruction `[0x1800,0x1804)`, not the entire retained first stage or pre-handoff record2 immutability. Secondary entry precedes record2 handoff and prefix entry. The mandatory core0 first-stage handoff is the final `mov pc,r2` at `0x6118`; it may follow record2 handoff, prefix entry or waiting polls, but must precede core0's held gate, delay entry or completion-store boundary. Its observation and context checks survive into the scoped interval, with every existing guard retained. Full replacement startup, hardware qualification and discriminating #136/#137 controls remain open; the #135 actual forbidden secondary-fetch control at `0x12960` is separately authentication-held. No new negative launcher or fault guest is accepted. Historical SDK97 first-stage and schema3 secondary results do not qualify the composition. See the [current witness frontier](../emulation/README.md#composed-startup-witness-schema-5).

## Task-storage source contract (#143)

The source boundary is helper `[0x725c6c,0x725dd0)`, its two literal words `[0x725dd0,0x725dd8)` and conditional caller `[0x725dd8,0x726098)`. Canonical original DAT SHA-256 is `43d67afa1a2bc9ae8481bc0acf02cb085ed0f515122dcbd26addcae102cd1490`; decoded image4 SHA-256 is `f2e2acab892dafeed85171f6870a411f0a2d5eae10921b86a24630d401dcff16`. Existing private `lz/id4.dis`, `decode/loader-40000.dis` and the identities recorded in E-SDBOOT-FILE-COVERAGE-150/E-LZ-GRAMMAR supply the binding. The artifact API requires whole-loader identities; it does not extract or digest the retained nine-word IRQ-mask service.

The helper takes a zero-based word index in r0, requested byte count in r1 and guest output-word address in r2. Its record is `0x40a0fc44 + 12*index`, with wrapping A32 address arithmetic, and contains `{base, requested_bytes, reserved}`. No helper index bound, nonzero-size check, size-alignment check or output-alignment check exists. Mapped target access semantics, reachable storage, code/stack separation and retained-service availability are conditional premises, not new input validation. Rust uses address newtypes and ordered A32 word accesses without constructing guest references; overlapping or unaligned guest words do not become Rust exclusive references.

| Helper path | Return | Ordered transition before retained release |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Base nonzero, saved requested size differs | `-33` | No transition writes |
| Base nonzero, matching size, reserved nonzero | `-18` | No transition writes |
| Base nonzero, matching size, reserved zero | `0` | Store the previously loaded base to output; store record reserved `1` |
| Base zero, unsigned wrapping `(cursor - 0x40a10200) + requested > 0x4b000` | `-33` | No transition writes |
| Base zero, capacity admitted | `0` | Store `cursor & ~0x40000000` to output; reload cursor; add `requested & ~3` with wrapping arithmetic; store cursor; reload output; store record base, original requested size and reserved `1`, in that order |

Output can alias cursor or any record word, including through the `0x40000000` alias bit. The second cursor load and later output load cannot be snapshotted before the preceding stores. An output/cursor alias changes both the cursor-addition input and the value eventually installed as record base. Reuse loads base before writing output, then stores reserved even when those addresses alias. Cursor arithmetic clears the low two requested-size bits; it does not round up. Capacity admission uses the unmasked request and unsigned modular arithmetic.

Contract `candidate_physical` clears guest bit `0x40000000` to name an expected alias. It is not observed physical backing; runtime mapping, TCM resolution and alias coherence remain qualification premises.

The retained helper prelude reads the selector service through `*(0x0096bd20)` plus `0x1618`, calls the selected vector's `+0x164` IRQ-mask service and saves its prior-mask result. It calls `0x427d4(0x409c280c, selector == 0 ? 1 : 2)` once and ignores its return. The retained epilogue calls `0x427fc(0x409c280c)` once, ignores that return and restores the prior mask through the selected vector's `+0x168`. Table slots are reloaded at their original sites. Neither this helper nor its caller proves acquisition success.

Retained `0x427d4` returns `-1` immediately when LDREX reads a nonzero lock; it retries internally only after failed STREX and executes `mcr p15,0,r2,c7,c10,5` on successful STREX. Retained `0x427fc` returns `-1` on an already-zero lock, otherwise attempts to store zero with the same STREX retry/barrier structure. Ignored acquisition failure can therefore be followed by a transition and release. The owned transition adds no retry, rollback or barrier. The A32 adapter consumes retained r4/r7/r8 arguments, calls Rust, places the result in r4 and branches to the unchanged epilogue. AAPCS preserves r5/r6/r10 holding lock, selector and prior mask. Caller `0x725e78` sets flags with `subs` before using them.

The conditional caller initially selects its service class using descriptor halfword zero minus one, then enters retained `0x7235d8`. Subsequent wrapper indexing uses descriptor full word zero minus one. Separate eight-byte records begin at `0x40a5b204`, with base at `+0` and requested size at `+4`. It reads size first, then base, and skips the helper when either is nonzero. When both are zero it stores descriptor word `+0xc` into wrapper size, reloads descriptor word zero, recomputes the index/output address, reloads the indexed wrapper size and calls the helper. Those reloads and the pre-error write remain retained. A descriptor alias can change the second index; there is no eager descriptor snapshot.

The task103 constructor at `0x69cef8..0x69cf34`, reached for stage4 input `4`, writes descriptor words `{103,0x781970,15,0x400,0x69cbf0,0}`. It does not initialize descriptor word `+0x18`. Class-table word `0x7caf34` is `2`. Absent intervening changes, the helper call has index `102`, request `0x400`, output/wrapper base `0x40a5b534`, wrapper size `0x40a5b538` and state `0x40a1010c`. The helper path remains conditional on the two wrapper reads. Direct helper execution cannot establish that natural path.

On helper failure the wrapper exits through `0x7236b0`, logs code `0x25` through `0x41a34`, stores the error at `0x40a0f82c`, calls the installed error callback through `0x723398` and returns the error if that callback returns. The earlier wrapper-size publication survives. On helper success or bypass, it calls the class-selected service slot `+0x80`, admitting only `0` and `-41`; other returns take its retained error path. It later reads wrapper size and base at `0x725f54/0x725f5c`, fills the pointed storage with the low task-id byte through `0x7221c8`, calls `0x42634`, passes the saved pair to service slot `+0`, restores the outer critical state and invokes selected slot `+0xc`. Nonzero returns from the latter two services are logged as code `3`, stored in the same error word and returned through the callback path. These later effects and errors are retained, not owned task creation.

Later release helper `0x726098` clears only state reserved at `0x7260fc`, retaining base and size. Caller `0x72623c` invokes that release after a retained service `+4` call even when that service returns an error. Subsequent reuse can observe the saved pair and cleared reservation. No pool reclamation, cursor reset or storage lifetime follows from that store alone. Indexed state, wrapper records, pool and cursor are separate from initializer descriptors. The image's initial cursor word at `0x89ea98` is `0x40a10200`; runtime cursor inputs, initial wrapper/state words, descriptor `+0x18`, alias mapping/coherence, concurrent writers and later lifetime remain unproved.

`task-storage-image` occupies exactly `[0x725cc4,0x725da8)` with no padding in the measured native artifact. Its six-instruction adapter is 24 bytes, Rust is 204 bytes and the emitted Rust frame is 24 bytes. That compile/link result is placement evidence, not public Haskell admission, semantic equivalence or a natural-caller witness. Derivation checks canonical identities, selected original words, linked symbols, exact adapter instructions, branch targets and the conditional emitted-frame proof. Decoded-image staging changes only that transition window; the original prelude/epilogue, literal pool, caller and surrounding decoded image remain byte-carried. Typed `StagedCandidate` delegates to shared `Emulation.Image4Staging`, reusing `//lz:lz-encode`, `//host/firmware:rebuild-update replace-record4` and `//lz:fflzd`. The shared gate checks carried-stream decode and outside-window preservation against the unchanged canonical DAT. Public host admission, candidate-DAT staging, runtime qualification, #140/#142 acceptance and installed-service behavior remain separate.

The staging CLI takes an explicit `SCRATCH_DIR` before `OUT_DAT OUT_CONTRACT`. Shared validation constructs an opaque `ScratchDirectory` under ignored named `private-data/scratch`. Temporary files and each child process's working directory/TMPDIR use that validated directory; the parent environment is unchanged.

Capacity arithmetic loads the original pool-base word at `0x725dd4` through linker symbol `xm1_task_storage_pool_base_literal`, after the cursor load. `canonicalSource` pins that word to `0x40a10200`; linked-artifact derivation pins its symbol address. Rust uses `sym` and A32 `ldr register, symbol`, with normal `R_ARM_LDR_PC_G0` relocation as specified by LLVM's [literal-load test](https://github.com/llvm/llvm-project/blob/llvmorg-21.1.8/llvm/test/MC/ARM/pcrel-ldr-relocs.s), [ELF writer](https://github.com/llvm/llvm-project/blob/llvmorg-21.1.8/llvm/lib/Target/ARM/MCTargetDesc/ARMELFObjectWriter.cpp) and [LLD encoder](https://github.com/llvm/llvm-project/blob/llvmorg-21.1.8/lld/ELF/Arch/ARM.cpp). The measured instruction at `0x725d3c`, word `0xe59f6090`, resolves to `0x725dd4`; cursor load `0x725d34` precedes it and subtraction `0x725d40` follows it. No post-link patch, native-source change or expanded window is part of the frame-admission work.

`//replacement/task_storage:task-storage-artifact-tests` submits synthetic A32 artifacts to public `deriveArtifact`. Each fixture has the ordinary adapter and symbol ABI; its body names an admission boundary for caller preservation, own-frame access, writeback, control or guest-memory capability. These fixtures are not firmware executions or successful runtime receipts. Regression-only source precedes the admission correction so authorized before/after runs can preserve the original result.

`artifactAdmission` exposes typed `Admission`, `Scratch`, `Access` and `Premise` metadata; staged contracts add `frame_admission` with status `conditional`. Scratch offsets are relative to incoming Rust SP, not claimed guest/physical backing. The emitted proof follows decoded A32 control and transfer effects, tracks incoming return and r4..r11 identities through allocated frame words, invalidates deallocated words, applies every writeback and requires restored SP/callee state at every return. It derives scratch from the body rather than expecting 24 bytes. Its conservative leaf subset refuses calls, control cycles, unsupported address arithmetic and bodies exceeding a 0x400-byte own-frame or 4096-context admission policy.

Frame addresses, scalar values and guest address capabilities are separate. Guest loads yield data, never new pointer authority. Source-derived capabilities cover `0x40a0fc44 + 12*incoming_index` with wrapping arithmetic and offsets 0/4/8, cursor `0x4089ea98`, incoming output r2's word, linked literals and retained pool literal `0x725dd4`. The proof remains generic over all input indices, sizes and output addresses; task103 metadata does not instantiate it. Output/state/cursor may alias, and no guest-alignment or input-bound validation is added.

Admission is conditional on mapped target word semantics, AAPCS incoming stack, live ordinary frame storage without outside writers, byte-stable image/literal lifetime and reached guest writes being disjoint from the linked image, adapter, retained literal and derived allocated frame. Typed root names do not discharge those exclusions. Runtime caller/stack provenance, alias coherence, physical/TCM backing, surrounding memory, initialization, concurrency and reserved-storage lifetime remain unresolved. This proof establishes neither helper return/effect equivalence nor a naturally executed reservation. The public malformed-artifact regressions and production correction have not been executed in this source-only increment.
