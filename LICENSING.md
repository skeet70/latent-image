### Unicorn is not covered by the project grant

The engine is not vendored, but `build-support/repositories.bzl` exposes its shared library to the bounded C runner, and the retained Rust/Python engine loads it in-process. [The pinned Nix engine declaration](https://github.com/NixOS/nixpkgs/blob/c7def046b9a883d46974757852106483d741586f/pkgs/by-name/un/unicorn/package.nix) identifies `GPL-2.0-only`. [Unicorn 2.1.4 README](https://github.com/unicorn-engine/unicorn/blob/2.1.4/README.md) describes GPLv2 and [COPYING](https://github.com/unicorn-engine/unicorn/blob/2.1.4/COPYING) supplies GPL version 2. [QEMU's component statement](https://github.com/unicorn-engine/unicorn/blob/2.1.4/qemu/LICENSE) identifies per-file terms; a GPL-2.0-or-later grant for some QEMU files does not establish that option for the whole engine.

Unicorn's [public header](https://github.com/unicorn-engine/unicorn/blob/2.1.4/include/unicorn/unicorn.h) names LGPL2 and [COPYING.LGPL2](https://github.com/unicorn-engine/unicorn/blob/2.1.4/COPYING.LGPL2) supplies the LGPL version-2 text. This is not the engine's license. The [pinned Nix Python wrapper declaration](https://github.com/NixOS/nixpkgs/blob/c7def046b9a883d46974757852106483d741586f/pkgs/development/python-modules/unicorn/default.nix) instead says GPL-2.0-or-later. Older inherited notes describe bindings as BSD; that description is not enough to establish terms for every installed binding or release. Preserve applicable per-file notices and resolve the component/version scope when distributing the wrapper. None of those wrapper or header terms upgrades the engine's GPLv2-only classification.

GNU documents the [GPLv2-only/GPLv3 incompatibility](https://www.gnu.org/licenses/gpl-faq.html#AllCompatibility). AGPLv3's section 13 permits combination with GPLv3, not a conversion of GPLv2-only engine code. Assess actual combinations of project code and the engine, distributed binaries, bundled Nix closures and deployed services. Linking or loading in-process does not by itself settle the combined-work question. A flake reference neither redistributes the engine nor automatically clears or blocks source-only publication; keeping the library outside Git does not settle the terms of a later combined artifact.

GPLv3 code, including the pinned patcher, is a different case: GPLv3 and AGPLv3 each permit a combined work under their section 13. The GPLv3 portion retains GPLv3, the AGPLv3 portion retains AGPLv3, and the network-source requirement applies to the combination. Preserve both licenses and all applicable notices. An external checkout or restricted VM alone does not prove mere aggregation; classify the actual exporter artifact if it is packaged with upstream inputs.

## Release decisions

Latent Image publishes source, tools and scoped findings at `skeet70/latent-image`, not proprietary inputs or the private research history. Source publication does not establish runtime verification or clear combined artifacts. The project license does not settle every third-party term or packaging obligation; the following scopes and obligations remain:

1. **x70view inherited closure:** Retain prominent credit to jitl (justjake) and x70view under the 2026-10-14 redistribution grant. Preserve upstream notices and distinguish inherited implementation from original AGPL software. The grant does not supply an author-provided SPDX license, sublicensing authority or downstream modification terms; resolve those terms before asserting them for recipients. These remaining questions do not negate the redistribution permission.
2. **Unicorn combined artifacts:** Assess the GPLv2-only engine boundary for actual combinations with AGPLv3 programs, distributed linked/load-in-process binaries, bundled Nix closures and deployed services. Where compatibility remains uncertain, obtain an artifact-specific legal assessment or use a compatible engine, a rights-holder grant or a reviewed separate-program architecture. A source-only tree's flake reference is neither automatic clearance nor an automatic publication blocker.
3. **Unicorn acceleration fork:** The viewer loads jitl (justjake)'s public fork at `ecf0e25a2a4c308655e30e1d3198d148c4fc0819` from `https://github.com/justjake/unicorn`, fetched by `build-support/unicorn-x70.nix` against a pinned source hash. Its patches leave Unicorn 2.1.4's `COPYING` and per-file QEMU terms in place, and the optional TCI files it adds keep their QEMU notices, so item 2 governs it as it governs the stock engine. Conveying its binary also conveys Corresponding Source for those patches.
4. **Proprietary material and captured fixtures:** Keep firmware, ROM, raw dumps, calibration, camera identities, raw logs, private manuals and captured images outside the release, except individually recorded scoped fixture dispositions. On 2026-10-14 the owner chose to retain the exact sixteen-byte remembered MCU packet in `emulation/xm1-upstream/emulator/captured_mcu_profile.py` publicly as an observed fixture. This disposition is not a general grant for Fujifilm material. Review other copied instruction sequences, tables, disassembly quotations and captured fixtures individually. Addresses, interface descriptions, protocol knowledge and original executable models remain publishable findings under [the publication policy](docs/principles.md#publication-and-asset-boundaries); firmware derivation alone is not an exclusion criterion. A firmware download page, purchased manual or another researcher's permission to use their own code is not a redistribution grant for Fujifilm material.
5. **Public history boundary:** The public repository has a separate root and does not inherit the private research history. Preserve the research repository and its complete history privately; do not transfer its Git object database, refs, reflogs or private artifacts, and do not use mirror/all-ref pushes. Import later reviewed source changes without merging private-history branches. Preserve upstream notices and attribution, and review current-tree privacy and the public commit identity independently of historical identity. Do not rewrite the existing private research history.
6. **Artifact obligations:** For the chosen release artifact, preserve upstream notices, selected dependency license texts, modification notices and Corresponding Source, including build/install scripts. Review bundled components and runtime closures, the viewer's legal notices and network-source offer, and any installation-information obligation before shipping binaries or providing a modified network service. This audit does not waive those obligations or certify a future build closure.
7. **FujiMod reference build:** jitl has stated FujiMod will be AGPL; no licence file at the pinned commit yet. jitl (justjake)'s [FujiMod](https://github.com/justjake/FujiMod) is pinned at [`ecdb6cb653ca0fa3372e4a4cf92c88bffa13ae29`](https://github.com/justjake/FujiMod/tree/ecdb6cb653ca0fa3372e4a4cf92c88bffa13ae29); its tree was checked on 2026-10-25. The statement does not specify an AGPL version or establish a grant for that commit. Until a grant lands, `build-support/fujimod.nix` fetches FujiMod by commit for local reference builds only, no FujiMod source is vendored here, and no binary built from it is conveyed. That includes the original AGPL `emulation/fujimod-xm1` driver, whose binary links FujiMod crates. Those binaries link its vendored Unicorn fork (`0c0e055c8895cf3c38aa751a57e5895508d8393f`) statically, so item 2 governs them, and `build-support/fujimod-unicorn-context-align.patch` is a local modification of that GPL-2.0-only code. FujiMod also contains commits by Josef Miegl; coverage of those contributions must be established by the eventual grant.

The historical metadata/content scan found no matches for the inspected credential-prefix or private-key-marker patterns and no non-synthetic firmware/media binary filenames among reachable objects. That limited result is not a claim that history contains no secrets or proprietary expression. Human review of captured text and copied material remains part of the release decision. The synthetic binaries in `emulation/fixtures/` have [authored provenance](emulation/fixtures/PROVENANCE.md), not a license grant for other `.bin` files.
