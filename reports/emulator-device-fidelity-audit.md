# FujiMod device-fidelity audit: cold boot to SHOOTING MENU

The kernel-timer direction error (`E-XM1-TIMER-IRQ-LAWS-150`, fixed by `fujimod-timer-upcount.patch`, now default) survived every independent review because qualifications compare stock against owned code on the same model, so a wrong device law cancels out. This audit checks the FujiMod device models the cold-boot-to-menu route touches in the named classes (timers, interrupt controller, DMA, display and scanout, storage and NAND, power and clock, inter-core, plus the boot inputs they depend on) against the X-M1 hardware evidence the repository holds: hardware-observed ledger records, console captures, RAM dumps and camera receipts under `private-data/`. Ledger: `E-EMULATOR-DEVICE-FIDELITY-AUDIT-150`.

Model: pinned FujiMod `3d90035d` with the default patch stack (source `/nix/store/ygqi0dd3677862nfkf9vh4mk78nvi5kp-fujimod-3d90035d`; crate paths below are relative to its `crates/`, `catalog/` to its root) and the run document `emulation/fujimod-xm1/data/xm1-150.json`. Route: the stock DAT and captured ROM, erased settings, no card (`emulation/README.md:1365,1371`, `E-FUJIMOD-XM1-FLASH-BOOT-150`). Nothing was run on an emulator or a camera. "Agreement" means a hardware-observed value matches the model; where that match passes through a static reading of the firmware, the verdict says so.

## 1. Result

Three laws besides the timer are contradicted by hardware reads: the boot-ROM extent (fixed, §3 item 1), the core-1 identity, and the elapsed time of early boot, which the console stores of one camera boot and both engines put 15-28 ticks apart. One Run-3 snapshot conflicts with the interrupt controller's `+0x1c0` law. The companion boot-status inputs and the GPIO input words match only parts of the hardware states the camera has shown, which were not cold cardless boots. Most laws on the route, including every interrupt-controller, DMA, display, NAND-controller and DRAM law, have no hardware evidence: they are declared, and a wrong one would cancel out as the timer did.

## 2. Devices

### 2.1 Timers (`timers`, `0xfff91000`)

- Mode `0x63` (boot, reload `0xffffffff`). Model: counts up, `(count + elapsed/64) mod (reload+1)`, no interrupt (`emulator-exr2-system/src/timer.rs:90-92,257-260`). Hardware: the lowest log tick is 21 in both read-log captures (`private-data/scratch/DeviceLaws/subtick-20261009T064945Z-read-log.txt`; laws report §6.3), and the timer switch seeds the tick cell from `count/1875` (static, `E-LOG-TICK-SOURCE-150`). A down-count from `0xffffffff` would seed about 2,290,649. Agreement on direction through that static reading.
- Mode `0x23` direction. Model after the patch: the register reads `reload − remaining` (`timer.rs:93-104,146-156`). Hardware (`E-XM1-TIMER-IRQ-LAWS-150`): `[+4] − [+0]` falls in all 942 + 3,062 same-tick pairs, values 6..29,881 and 1..29,924. Agreement. The range `0..reload−1` and the value at a wrap are the patch's declared assumptions (`timer.rs:16`; laws report U1).
- Rate. Model: divider `1 << ((control>>4)&7)` (`timer.rs:141-144`), 4 virtual ticks per count in `0x23`. The run document declares no board timebase; its companion RTC and touch nodes declare 12,000,000 ticks per second (`xm1-150.json:470,1977`), so 120,000 ticks per 10 ms kernel tick at that rate. Hardware: 14,958 ticks in 149.759 s, 99.88 Hz (`reports/console-trace-instrument.md:93`, `reports/input-map-150.md:33`). Agreement within 0.12% on the ratio; the input clock itself is model policy (U3).
- Elapsed time at the switch. The switch sets the tick cell to `count/1875` (`0x40be8`, laws §6.2) while the domain clock starts at 0 and advances by 10 per tick (`reports/auto-act-arming.md:194`), so domain clock = 10 × (tick − seed). Body Run-3 (`E-BULK-READ-RUN3-150`, `private-data/sessions/20261025T050355Z-read-bulk/ram-00000000-05aa0000.bin`): domain-A clock `0x0005476c` = 2,448,190, tick cell `0x000c0800` = 244,835, seed 16. The FujiMod and retained calibration dumps (`private-data/ram-calibration-{fujimod,retained}/ram-00000000-05aa0000.bin`) hold exactly 10 × tick (25,460/2,546 and 23,720/2,372), seed 0. The capture is not atomic, but the two words are 432 KiB apart, about 75 ms or 7.5 ticks at Run-3's 5,933,421 B/s, short of 16. Model: 24 instructions per virtual tick (`xm1-150.json:7-11`) and the switch at about 6.14M-6.43M entered instructions (adoption report §2). The console store of the same boot confirms the offset (next item).
- Boot log ticks, host-only measurement. The console store at `0x400a0800` (physical `0xa0800` in each dump) was extracted from Run-3 and both calibration dumps and decoded with `//console-trace:trace-census` (`private-data/scratch/boot-elapsed/`). All three begin with the same boot records: four type-`0x09` thread-3 records, then type `0x0f` sends from threads `0x5f`, `0x22`, `0x06` and `0x3b`. The body's first record is at tick 15 and the FujiMod and retained stores' at tick 0. Matching the first occurrence of each type, thread and argument: body 21/23/29/31/37/54/60 against FujiMod 0/1/5/6/16/27/32 for the same seven records, so the offset grows from 15 at the first record to 28 by the thread-`0x02` burst at body tick 60. The retained store matches FujiMod's ticks within 1 through that point. Both engines therefore run early boot about 15 ticks (150 ms) short before the first log record and about 45% short in tick terms over the next 45 body ticks. All three boots are ordinary no-card boots of RC0 with stored `0xf7 = 1` ([RAM calibration](ram-calibration.md)); the body's settings and calibration differ from the engines' erased store, which can change later records but not the shared opening sequence [INFERENCE]. Verdict: disagreement on boot elapsed time, from one body boot; the read-log captures' first records at tick 21 agree with a non-zero offset. Hardware boot waits the model completes early (NAND, PHY, power) would account for it [INFERENCE].
- Where the boot gap lies, host-only analysis. Matching the opening records puts most of it before the timer switch: the camera seeds the tick cell with 15-16 ticks (about 150-160 ms of mode-`0x63` time since the boot records' last counter reset), the model with 0, under 10 ms. After the switch the camera spends 39 ticks between its first type-`0x0f` send (tick 21) and the thread-`0x02` burst (tick 60), FujiMod 32 on the down-counting timer. Before the switch, boot record 1 fills image 4's BSS (3,485,184 bytes) and decompresses image 4 (a 1,336,679-byte record into 2,631,680 bytes) (`reports/firmware-extraction.md:227-231`, static), and the two loaders and image 4's record are read from NAND (1,448,679 bytes). The model's declared rates make that fast: NAND reads `max(2, bytes/512)` ticks (`emulator-exr2-storage/src/nand.rs:157-162`), LZ input `max(2, bytes/512)` ticks (`emulator-exr2-codecs/src/lz.rs:76,275`), memory-DMA fill `max(2, bytes/32)` ticks (`memory.rs:161`), so at 12,000,000 ticks per second about 0.24 ms of NAND, 0.2 ms of LZ and 9 ms of fill; DRAM PHY training completes at once (`dram.rs:94-131`). Whether all of these fall after the last counter reset is not settled: records 1 and 2 both zero the counter (`0x26d8`, `0x2308`; laws report §6) and their relative order on two cores is not traced. No camera record observes this window except the seed itself, so the one hardware number is an aggregate of about 150 ms that the declared device rates cannot reach [INFERENCE: camera NAND and LZ throughput are unmeasured].
- Post-switch window on the current default, one emulated run (`E-BOOT-POST-SWITCH-UPCOUNT-150`). A FujiMod cold cardless boot at main `d8b19056` (up-count timer, widened ROM decode, core-1 `0x8003`, unanswered CMD53), captured by `//emulation:ram-calibration capture fujimod` at the post-ff80 pause and decoded the same way, gives the same opening ticks as the down-counting calibration dump: first type-`0x0f` send at tick 0, thread-`0x02` burst at tick 32, and every first-occurrence record through that burst at the same tick except `0f 3b 0x73003b63` (6 before, 7 now). The window is therefore 32 ticks on the current default against the camera's 39, 7 ticks (about 70 ms) short, and the body-minus-model offset still grows from 15-16 at the first records to 28 by body tick 60. The seed stays 0 (domain clock `0x60b8` = 10 × tick cell `0x9ac`). The µs busy-waits the timer change lengthened do not move these ticks; what fills the camera's extra 7 is not identified.
- Channels 1-2 (`+0x10..+0x2c`): polled up-counters after reload `~0`, application mode otherwise (`timer.rs:431-433,508-533`). Channel 3: application only, `+0x3c` refuses. First-period phase after the reload write (`timer.rs:478-482`): declared. No hardware evidence for any of these (laws report U2, §6.3).

### 2.2 Interrupt controller (`irq0` `0xfffb3000`, `irq1` `0xfffb4000`)

- `+0x1c0`. Model: reads the top active line, or `0xffff` when none is active (`irq.rs:381-385`). Stock acknowledge `0x430a8` reads `+0x200`, scans the active bitmap, then stores `{[+0x1c0], line}` at `0x40054500 + 8·priority` (`private-data/analysis/x-m1-1.50/decode/loader-40000.dis` lines 3100-3155); only init (`0x428c0`) and EOI (`0x432b4`) otherwise write the slots, both with `{0, 0xffffffff}`. Body Run-3 holds `{0x00000000, 0x0000005e}` at slot 7 with nesting counter `0x000545a4` = 1; the FujiMod calibration dump holds `{0x45, 0x45}` at slot 13 (line 69, the LZ decoder), counter 0. Under the model a slot always carries `{line, line}`, so the body's word 0 = 0 conflicts with it. The body's handler packet for line 94 at `0x000541cc` is the default (`0x42840`, priority 15), not priority 7, so the slot may be stale from a redefinition between acknowledge and EOI, which EOI would clear at the reloaded priority 15 instead. Verdict: conflicting single snapshot, not a settled disagreement. Line 94 is not named on X-M1, and the run document has no source on it.
- Enables `+0x80`/`+0xa0`, mode fields `+0x60/+0x100/+0x140/+0x180`, priority sense and tie order (`irq.rs:323-351,420-436`), acknowledge and spurious return (`irq.rs:387-410`), EOI pop (`irq.rs:454-463`), software pend and line 120 (`irq.rs:423-431`), line-32 fan-out to both cores (`xm1-150.json:86-89`), WFI wake (`timer.rs:295`, `irq.rs:610-611`): no hardware evidence (laws report U4-U11). Lower-value-wins agrees with stock priorities (static only). The spurious-acknowledge counter `0x000544fc` is 0 on the body and both engines.

### 2.3 Memory DMA and LZ decoder

- Memory DMA (`0xfff02000`, `0xfff02300`; `emulator-exr2-serial/src/dma/memory.rs`): three accepted configurations (loader fill without interrupt, application fill and copy with interrupt `0x5b`) (`:111-157`), completion after `max(2, bytes/32)` ticks (`:161`), status `+0x24` 1 while busy and `0x40` when done (`:342-363`), abort by writing 8 (`:312-316`). The route uses it for the image-4 BSS fill before image 4 starts and the `[0x01460000,0x0171be40)` fill before image 5 (`reports/firmware-extraction.md:227-231`, `E-FFF02000-CALLSITE-150`, static). Hardware: none. The bit-6 completion poll agrees with static reading only; the BSS is rewritten at runtime, so Run-3 cannot test the fills.
- LZ decoder (`lz`, `0xfff43000`; `emulator-exr2-codecs/src/lz.rs`, interrupt line 69): decodes image 4 on every boot. Hardware: the loaded images equal the DAT decodes apart from listed runtime bytes (`E-RAM-PROBE-150`, `E-DUMP-RAM-150`), and image 4 differs from the body in 80 bytes on both engines (`reports/ram-calibration.md`). Agreement on output; timing and interrupt delivery have no hardware evidence.

### 2.4 Display: timing, scanout, formatter

- Display timing (`0xfff6a000`, `0xfff6d000`; `emulator-exr2-display/src/timing.rs`; `frame_ticks` 400,000, about 30 Hz at 12 MHz [INFERENCE]): three phase interrupts per frame on lines `0x34/0x34/0x35` and `0x37/0x37/0x38` (`:55,131-133,271-273`), status `+0x30` bit 1 set at phase 0 and cleared at phase 1 (`:229-236`). Hardware: none; no ledger record measures panel refresh or phase order, and the 30 fps install video (`E-FILMSIM-INSTALL-150`) cannot resolve it. This status polarity is the display analogue of the timer direction: a value the firmware reads that hardware never confirmed.
- Scanout (`0xfff6b000`, `0xfff6e000`; `emulator-exr2-display/src/scanout.rs`): register file with a 2-tick reset handshake (`:153-194`); pixel packing yycbcr, BT.601 full, `mode1-abgr8888-straight`, and backdrop and rectangle profiles declared "unmeasured" in the run document. Hardware: the Run-3 preview buffer decodes as 4:2:2 BT.601 full (`reports/xm1-snapshot-producers.md:172`), which is upstream of composition and a visualization choice, not a scanout measurement. No hardware evidence.
- Display record `0x957aa0`, host-only comparison for this audit (`cmp -l` over `[0x957aa0,+0x3000)`): FujiMod differs from the body in 40 bytes, retained in 39. In both `[+0x00,+0x3c)` and `[+0x12dc,+0x12fc)` the body holds zeros where both engines hold nonzero words, and `+0x2c14` reads `0x24` on the body and `0x22` on both engines. Interpretation open: the body was captured over ff80 after the shooting screen, and these fields are not decoded. The first 1 KiB of shadow `0x96b9bc` differs in 64 bytes on each engine.
- Formatter (`0xfff69000`, `0xfff6c000`; `emulator-exr2-display/src/formatter.rs`): bit-28 strobe latches after 2 ticks (`:150-160,241-255`). No hardware evidence; static sources also leave it open (`reports/render-service-manifest-draft.md:72`).

### 2.5 Boot ROM, NAND and SD

- Boot ROM content: the run document requires sha256 `4ecedfbf…` (`xm1-150.json:2933`), the hash of the camera capture (`E-BOOTROM-PROBE-150`, `E-BOOTROM-X70-IDENTITY-150`). Agreement.
- Boot ROM extent. Hardware: `0x80004000-0x80007fff` is byte-identical to the first 16 KiB (`E-BOOTROM-DISCRIMINATOR-150`), and 256-byte reads at `0x80008000`, `0x80010000` and `0x80100000` return the image's first 256 bytes (`E-BOOTROM-DISCRIMINATOR-150`, `E-STRAP-READ-150`). The catalog declares one 16 KiB `boot-rom` region at `0x80000000` (`catalog/soc/exr2/soc.json:72-78`), a 16 KiB bus map (`dma-map-03`, `:1446-1455`) and one 16 KiB identity map per core (`cpu0-map-07`, `:1274-1283`; `cpu1-map-15`, `:1360-1370`). Since fix 1 the X-M1 adapter widens all four to `[0x80000000,0x80104000)` and fills them with the repeated image (`emulation/fujimod-xm1/src/run.rs`, `BOOT_ROM_DECODE_BYTES`); beyond `0x80104000` is unmeasured. The route does not depend on it: the reset copy `ldm r2!` at `0x80000104` starts from `r2 = 0x00003ff8`, a CPU low-alias address, so its last 24 bytes come from CPU `0x4000`, which both cores map to DRAM physical `0x4000` (`cpu0-map-02`, `cpu1-map-10`), and only the first copied word survives the following zero-fill (`reports/bootrom-window.md:239`). What the camera maps at CPU `0x4000` at reset is unmeasured. A strict board refuses an unmapped access, so no completed route read physical `[0x80004000,0x80104000)` before the widening.
- Boot-source latch `0xfff98000`: resets to 1 (`emulator-exr2-system/src/boot.rs:36-55`; `xm1-150.json:772`); the camera reads `0x19`, bits 2:0 = 1 (`E-STRAP-READ-150`). Agreement on the selector; bits 31:5 before the ROM write-back are unobserved.
- NAND medium. Model: `DAT[604..]` placed linearly, 2,048-byte pages, 64 pages per block, no bad blocks, spare and unwritten bytes `0xff` (`emulation/fujimod-xm1/src/run.rs:141-144`, `xm1-150.json:2590-2601`, `emulator-devices/src/parts/nand.rs:341-379`). Hardware: loaded images equal the DAT decodes (`E-RAM-PROBE-150`, `E-DUMP-RAM-150`); the segment table at `0x0061c000` matches flash apart from five runtime-written bytes (`E-SEGTABLE-RUNTIME-150`), and the RAM calibration finds the segment-table window identical and low boot RAM different in 11 bytes (`reports/ram-calibration.md`, `E-RAM-CALIBRATION-150`); blocks 0-14 map identity (`E-RECOVERY-PHYSICAL-PAIR-STATUS13-150`); 131,072-byte banks read at raw blocks `0x3ac` and `0x3b2` (`E-AREA2-BANKS-150`, `E-FILMSIM-READBACK-150`). Agreement as the loaders see it. Raw firmware-region bytes, the 528-byte sector stride and the 1,024-block capacity are static only.
- NAND controller `0xfff52000` (`emulator-exr2-storage/src/nand.rs`): every completion clean, status `0xc8` = 0 and `0x54` bit 0 set (`:535-540`), ECC always clean (`:2`), reads `max(2, bytes/512)` ticks (`:157-162`), erase 64 ticks (`xm1-150.json:1369`). Hardware: none; the raw-media read gates the spare area off (`E-RAW-MEDIA-READ-150`) and no NAND operation has been timed.
- SD host (`0xfff50000`, adapter `emulation/fujimod-xm1/src/sd_host.rs`). Card detect GPIO 12.7 active-low (`sd_host.rs:96-97,376-378`) agrees with the detected-card read of `0x28` (`E-SD-SLOT-DAMAGE-150`; `E-CARD-DETECT-LIVE-150` reads present from an empty but damaged slot); an empty undamaged slot has never been read. The cardless route sends no SD command (the adapter refuses one while absent, `:594-597`). Post-boot hardware words read `0x00000003` with the slot empty (`E-SDHOST-IDLE-READ-150`) against the model's present-state `0x00020000` (`:903`); the cardless route does not read them [INFERENCE], so this matters for card routes only.

### 2.6 Power, clock, reset, DRAM, inter-core, inputs

- Power hold `0xfff88084` (`emulator-devices/src/exr2/clock.rs:21-33,186-226`): a write with bit 0 clear starts a 100,000-tick deadline when none is pending, a write with bit 0 set cancels it, and a deadline that comes due ends the run with "supply off"; nothing else arms or disarms it. One observed stock cold boot to SHOOTING MENU (`E-POWER-HOLD-WRITERS-150`) logs four accesses to the word, all on core 0 before the first stop at 80,000,000 instructions: the ROM init stores 1 (CPU-view PC `0xec`, ROM `0x800000ec`, tick 2), the configuration apply stores 0 (PC `0x330`, tick 193, which arms the deadline for tick 100,193), and boot record 1 reads 0 and stores 1 at listing offsets `0x26ec`/`0x26f4` at tick 2,676, which disarms it 2,483 ticks after the release. No other access follows through the menu. The record-1 store forms its address as `mvn r3,#0x77000` (`0xfff88fff`) with offset `-0xf7b`, so the earlier scan for references to `0xfff88000-0xfff880ff` missed it, and `E-SDBOOT-NOTE-HOLD-BINDING-150`'s "no later code of an ordinary boot sets it again" does not hold for boot record 1. The model's law is consistent with the camera staying on; no fix is proposed. The 100,000-tick limit stays unmeasured, and the header's 13,430 ticks is not this route's 2,483.
- Reset words `0xfffb2004-0xfffb2014` (`emulator-exr2-system/src/reset.rs:22-30,69-87`): plain storage. No hardware evidence.
- DRAM controller and PHY (`emulator-exr2-system/src/dram.rs:7-131`): PHY training completes as soon as `+0` bit 0 is written. The camera passes the ROM's wait on `0xfff01090` bit 28 every boot, which shows only that the bit sets. No hardware evidence for values or timing; the 512 MiB backing is not contradicted by reads ending at `0x0b38cdff` (`E-BULK-READ-BOUND-150`, static).
- Core identities (`catalog/soc/exr2/soc.json:6-60`): the catalog declares core 0 `0x4003`, core 1 `0x5003` (`cp14_identity` 20483, `:50`). The camera's RAM-push INSTALL mailbox reports service core `0x4003` and peer core `0x8003` (`E-RAM-PUSH-INSTALL-150`). Since fix 2 the X-M1 adapter configures `0x4003`/`0x8003` (`emulation/fujimod-xm1/src/run.rs`, `CoreIdentities`), and receipt consumers admit either pair as the model a receipt was recorded on. Stock code and owned code test only `== 0x4003`, so the route probably does not depend on the core-1 value [INFERENCE]; receipt and snapshot bytes change.
- Core-1 release (`emulator-exr2-system/src/core_release.rs:57-66`, line 120 on `irq1`): release trigger, start PC `0x3fff0000` and TCM state are static or emulated only (`E-BOOT-RELEASE-PATH-150`, `E-PEER-STARTUP-OBSERVED-SHAPE-150`). Run-3's loader-B tick root at `0x000e4588` matches both engines in its first 32 bytes, which shows the same initialization, not the release law.
- Companion MCU (`serial_spi1`, `xm1-150.json:452-635`): boot status `0x60` = `0x10` and header `0x50-0x5f` = `00 00 00 00 d0 07 00 ff 00 80 00 04 00 00 aa fc`. Hardware: `0x10` only after a jig-session ending and `0x12` after an ordinary power-off (`E-BOOT-REASON-CORRELATION-150`, `E-JIG-CYCLE-D-150`); header bytes 0-3 read `0f 0c c3 03` with checksum `1b` after a switch ending, and byte 6 reads 1 after an ordinary boot (`E-JIG-CYCLE-D-150`). The model matches neither state in full. The RTC value has no hardware evidence.
- GPIO input words (`emulator-devices/src/exr2/gpio.rs:253-255`, `xm1-150.json:171-188`): port 0 = `0xe6c`, port 2 = `0x1200`, port 3 = `0x308`, port 12 = `0x80`, others 0. Hardware (`E-STRAP-READ-150`, a jig power-up with USB attached and a card inserted): live port 0 `0xd4e`. Bits 0, 2, 3, 4, 6, 7, 10 and 11 agree; bits 1, 5, 8 and 9 differ (camera 1, 0, 1, 0; model 0, 1, 0, 1). The reset-stage mirror words at `0x400a0000` for ports 1-15 read `0, 0x2ff, 0x300, 0x3dc2, 4, 0x3e, 4, 0x80, 0, 0, 8, 0x28, 0xc0, 0, 0xf` (`reports/bootrom-window.md:250`) against mostly zero in the model; port 12 differs by the inserted card. The sample is a different state from the cold cardless route, so this is a conflicting different-state sample; no ordinary cardless read exists.
- Fn GPIO input law: `fn` drives GPIO0 bit 11 low during its board-tick hold and high on release, with scanner row 13 required to latch before the hold ends and its debounced release observed afterward. The typed `FN_INPUT` binding lives in `emulation/fujimod-xm1/src/guest.rs`; the canonical `xm1-150.json` run document is unchanged. X-M1 1.50 image 4, `id4-0061c400.bin` file offset `0x20be8c` (address `0x0082828c`), contains `28 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 02 00 00 0b 00 00 00 01 00 00 00 02 00 00 00 00 00 00 00`: code `0x28`, kind 2, GPIO operand `0x0000000b`, asserted halfword `+0x12 = 0`, debounce threshold `+0x14 = 2`. `id4.dis:0x006a0894..0x006a08f4` reads the kind, port/pin operand and asserted halfword, samples `0xfff89014 + port*0x40` and stores the equality result at row `+0x16`; `0x006a2fd0..0x006a2ff0` debounces it into `+0x15`, and `0x006a3050..0x006a3058` publishes code `+0x80` on release. `key_sample_inputs` (`0x006a0888`) and `key_update_repeat` (`0x006a2fa8`) are exact at decomp revision `f659f02b850fcba58de0f9b09ee24ad09a24c2f4` (`app/drivers/input/key_sample_inputs.c`, `key_update_repeat.c`). This electrical input law has no hardware evidence yet. Part A of the combined camera session can confirm it with the GPIO mirror read; no camera operation was performed for this bridge addition.

### 2.8 Wi-Fi SDIO module (`sdio`, `0xfff51000`)

Not on the cold boot to the menu: the Run-3 camera dump holds the WLAN driver state byte `0x014be62c` = 0 at the shooting screen, so the camera had not started Wi-Fi there, and the FujiMod calibration dump agrees. Opening PC AUTO SAVE SETTING > SIMPLE SETUP, menu (83,177), starts it; the property-pair rerun stopped there on both roles (`private-data/scratch/PropertyService/natural-3/`; `reports/recipe-bank-ui-ownership.md:997`).

- Firmware, static (plain image listing `private-data/analysis/x-m1-1.50/filmsim/plain.dis`): `WLAN_Start` `0x013e40cc` runs host and card init (CMD5/3/7/52) and probe `0x012af3ac`, whose module reset `0x01292024` issues the first CMD53 at `0x0129216c`: byte-mode write to function 1 register `0x8c` of `0x8000`, command word `0x353a0001`, argument `0x90011804`, DMA from `0x014760c0`. The wait `0x0128fb48` ends on transfer complete or on error status `& 0x17ff`; the request path `0x012918e8`/`0x01291884` turns every CMD53 failure into success. The reset then writes `0x8c` = 0, sleeps, and polls a CMD53 read of `0x8c` for bit `0x800` up to 512 times before returning `0xC0000001`; the probe fails and `WLAN_Start` returns −255 through an empty logging stub.
- Model: the module part `crates/emulator-parts-io/src/wifi_module.rs` declares CCCR bytes and, in protocol `rom_download` only, a reset, size and firmware-download register machine; the X-M1 run document selects `enumeration`, under which the pinned host refused every CMD53 and stopped the machine. The retained engine's `wifi_sdio.py` profiles bind addresses outside the X-M1 plain image.
- Hardware evidence: none for the module's answers. The service manual names the module DWM-W094 and the FCC filing shows a different part (`E-RAW-ENTRY-SOURCES-150`); no capture reads `0xfff51000` or records the driver after Wi-Fi start.
- Change, declared assumption (`fujimod-wifi-unanswered-cmd53.patch`, `E-WIFI-CMD53-UNANSWERED-150`): under `enumeration`, a four-byte byte-mode DMA CMD53 to function 1 (`0x353a0001` or `0x353a0011`) in the Transfer state completes with a command timeout (error status bit 0) and no data; block, PIO and other forms still stop the machine. The driver polls `0x30`/`0x32` directly (wait `0x0128fb48`), leaves on the error bit and masks the failure; its reset poll reads no `0x800` from the untouched buffer, and Wi-Fi start fails without a retry. A command timeout is the only outcome that asserts nothing about the module, since R5 flags, data errors or a zero-response success would each mean the module answered. A module that answers would need its register map and firmware image, which nothing in the repository measures for X-M1.
- To settle it: a camera read of the WLAN driver state after opening SIMPLE SETUP, or of `0x014760c0` after a reset poll, would show whether the real module answers.

### 4.1 Proposed combined camera session (fixes 3, 4 and 6 with U1, U6 and U14)

One ordinary-boot session on XM1-body-01 (stored `0xf7 = 1`, E-STORED-F7-COMMIT-RUN-150), proposal only; it runs after the owner's go. Power the body from the dummy battery with no card and no USB cable, boot to the shooting screen, wait 10 s, then attach USB (`ff80`). Parts A and B use `read-ram` only: function-2 reads, no config, gate or RAM write. Part C is the U1 RAM-push and is a RAM write.

- A, one pass (`read-ram PLAN_JSON`): `0x400a0000` 64 bytes, the boot-time GPIO port mirror taken before USB attach (fix 4); `0x400a00c0` 16 bytes and `0x400a00d0` 16 bytes, boot status `0x60` and the companion header after an ordinary power-off (fix 3); `0x400a0834` 256 bytes, the first 16 console-store slots of this boot, a second camera datum for the boot elapsed time (fix 5); `0x400541cc` 24 bytes, line 94's handler packet (§2.2). `read-ram` admits only the DRAM ranges `[0,0x20000000)` and `[0x40000000,0x60000000)` (`transport/FujiAF/ReadRam.hs:90`), so the live port registers `0xfff89014 + n·0x40` stay with `probe-straps`, which needs a maintenance boot and its own go.
- B, repeated (`read-ram PLAN_JSON --repeat 900 --interval-ms 100`): `0x40054500` 128 bytes and `0x400545a4` 4 bytes, the per-priority IRQ save slots and nesting counter (U6, fix 6). 900 passes of 2 requests stay under the 2032-request cap; any pass with the counter at 2 or more shows which slots nest, and repeated `{0, line}` slots decide between the model's `+0x1c0` law and a stale-slot reading.
- C (U1, laws report §8): RAM-push of the reviewed sampler that, with IRQ masked, reads `0xfff91004` once and `0xfff91000` 4096 times into a RAM buffer, then `read-ram` of that buffer. It writes camera RAM, so it needs its own review and go and can be left for a later session.
- D (U14, CPU-to-timer rate), in the same RAM-push as C. FujiMod's `instructions_per_tick` is 24 per board tick (`xm1-150.json:8-11`), with 4 board ticks per `0x23` counter step. Under the Exact policy the 24 are shared by all runnable cores (FujiMod `3d90035d` `crates/emulator/src/board/mod.rs:796-806,1111-1130`): 96 instructions per step for one runnable core, 48 per core for two. It is declared policy with no hardware figure, and it sets how many instructions every µs busy-wait and device delay costs; which core runs when is set separately by `quantum`. C's loop alone measures mostly peripheral-read latency. What D needs:
  - Privilege and masking checked. After masking IRQ, the sampler reads back CPSR and records its mode and I bit; a User-mode push cannot mask, and the result is then void. Each masked span stays under one 30,000-count period, and IRQ is unmasked between spans.
  - Fixed instruction classes. The loop bodies are fixed A32 sequences, with per-iteration counts taken from the sampler's disassembly: C's tight loop; a dependent `ADD` chain of K = 64 and K = 1024 between counter reads, which cannot dual-issue; an independent mix of the same K, which can (Cortex-R4 dual issue, ARM DDI 0363E §14.23); and K loads from a warmed buffer. The cost per instruction comes from the per-iteration deltas between K values (minimum and median), which also shows whether cost is linear in K.
  - Placement and caches recorded or controlled. The code, buffer and stack addresses and their region: the cached alias below `0x40000000`, the uncached alias above it, or TCM (MPU programming, `E-XM1-MPU-BOOT-RECORD-150`). Also SCTLR's cache-enable bits, CCSIDR for both caches, and ACTLR (dual-issue control). There is one discarded warm-up pass, and the load buffer sits in the cached range, smaller than the data cache.
  - Peer core state controlled. Each variant runs with the other core parked in the push's spin, which counts as runnable in the model, and, where the push can arrange it, with the other core running its own code. Core identity is read from CP14 (`0x4003` or `0x8003`); core 1 already runs RAM-push code on silicon (`E-RAM-PUSH-INSTALL-150`).
  - Optionally, the performance monitor (ARM DDI 0363E chapter 6): the cycle counter, event `0x08` (instruction architecturally executed) and `0x5e` (dual-issued pair) around each span. This only works in a privileged mode; PMNC.D is forced to 0 and PMNC is saved, recorded and restored. PMNC[23:16] records the core type, which no capture has read. Whether `0x08` counts both halves of a dual-issued pair is checked against the disassembly count.
  - Result: instructions per counter step per variant, per core and per peer state. Each run samples one core, so the model's equivalent, the sum over runnable cores per 4 ticks, is r(core 0, peer parked) + r(core 1, peer parked) from separate runs, which assumes the cores' rates are independent of which one samples. A span with the peer not runnable is not measured. Whether to set a mean, a load-dependent or a per-core rate is a model decision taken afterwards, with the routes that depend on timing re-qualified.
- Exit: cut the dummy-battery supply (E-JIG-CYCLE-D-150). Expected results: fix 3 profile bytes, fix 4 cardless boot port words, the fix 6 `+0x1c0` verdict, one more boot-seed sample, the U1 counter range and the U14 instructions per counter step.

## 5. Retained engine timer

The retained `emulation/xm1-upstream` engine counts mode `0x23` down as FujiMod did (`emulator/interrupts.py:343,690-695`). It is left unchanged. The code change is small: map the main counter read to `reload − (count or reload)` while `timer_control == 0x23` and `timer_reload != 0`, in `read()` offset 0 (`interrupts.py:241-242`) and in the three main-counter returns of `native_read()` (`interrupts.py:337,340,343`), leaving `tick()` state and mode `0x63` unchanged; about ten lines. What it would take beyond that:

- Re-baselining. The retained engine carries 10 of the 12 gating `verify-candidate` routes (`emulation/VerifyCandidate.hs:93-94,185-187`: flash-boot-check, three RAM-push, three flash-candidate, USB-toggle E8 and two updater toggles; native volatile and native SD run the bounded native suite). After the switch every busy-wait lengthens, so those 10 baselines under `private-data/verify-candidate/baselines/` would change together and every `verify-candidate` run would fail until all 10 are re-recorded and independently reviewed. Recorded wall times at 8 slots: emulator routes alone 1,021 s, a full run 2,194 s (`emulation/README.md:2315,2319`); at the 2-slot host budget they are longer. The retained-route evidence and checkpoints past the switch would need the same treatment as FujiMod's (adoption report §2).
- Policy. The engine is frozen legacy (`docs/principles.md:21`) and project-authored Python is not permitted (`AGENTS.md`). Changing it needs a maintainer exception; the alternative is to retire the gating routes to FujiMod, which the runtime direction already plans.

Proposed issue: decide between a scoped exception (patch, then re-baseline the 10 retained gating routes in one granted session) and leaving the retained timer wrong until the gating cutover, with the mismatch recorded as a limitation of every retained-engine result that depends on timer values or µs waits.
