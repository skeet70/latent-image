# Stock autofocus pilot protocol

Status: ready offline protocol draft, **no observations and no camera authorization**. Evidence label: `static-inference`, scoped to experimental design derived from [AF requirements](../spec/05-autofocus.md), [benchmark requirements](../spec/06-benchmarks.md), and [artifact contracts](../spec/08-artifact-and-runtime-contracts.md). Body, lens, firmware, supported controls, settings, and measurement repeatability remain unknown. This document and [machine manifest](../manifests/benchmark-protocol.json) define the pilot; they do not accept G0, G1, or G6. [Protocol schema](../schemas/benchmark-protocol.schema.json) validates the narrow v1 design, not session readiness or camera safety.

A session runs under [the camera-run procedure](../spec/07-governance-and-handoff.md#31-procedure). No transport request, automatic enumeration, lens command, service mode, opening, or firmware change is needed to prepare this protocol. Raw identity, photographs, video, settings, and session journals belong in private-data with verified durable backups; public outputs use redacted aliases and hashes.

## Scope and readiness

Use one observed X-M1 body/lens/firmware combination, static fixtures, ordinary still-photography AF, and stock controller only. The initial pilot is 11 scene/start combinations × 10 repeats = 110 scheduled attempts. It diagnoses measurement trouble and large regressions; it cannot establish precise p95, rare failure rates, or a speedup. Candidate and continuous/video AF performance are future claims.

Before collection, create a private resolved snapshot recording identity, lens/camera firmware, focal length, aperture, exposure mode/shutter/ISO, AF mode/region/size, AF assist, pre-AF behavior, processing, lighting setup, staging distances, target plane/ROI, and external video configuration. Verify supported ordinary controls rather than assuming any lens position telemetry or manual focusing behavior. Resolve all manifest nulls relevant to capture, start recipes, quality, and timing. Pin source revision, devenv.lock, MODULE.bazel.lock, configuration and protocol/report hashes. The public draft remains evidence of unknowns; never fill it with private identifiers.

Mount the camera and targets repeatably and document geometry. Use fixed illumination and exposure within a fixture where practical; if automatic exposure is needed, freeze its policy and record each actual exposure. Keep AF-region size, processing, aperture, focal length, assist and pre-AF matched across future paired controllers. Disable pre-AF through ordinary controls only if supported and approved; otherwise describe its action and include any prior focusing work in the timed task or limit the claim explicitly. No hidden pre-focus advantage is admissible.

## Fixtures and starts

| Scene | Lighting/content | Starts | Attempts |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| P01 | Bright, static high-contrast target | near, far, near-focused | 30 |
| P02 | Indoor, static textured target | near, far | 20 |
| P03 | Dim, same class of static textured target under fixed low illumination | near, far | 20 |
| P04 | Low-contrast static target under recorded illumination | near, far | 20 |
| P05 | Intended foreground target with a distracting textured background | near, far | 20 |

These are fixture categories, not assertions about the X-M1. In P05, visible hunting and wrong-plane selection are possible outcomes, not promised behavior. Light levels and distances are deliberately unfilled; measure or describe a reproducible controlled setup rather than inventing lux or motor units.

`near` means focus staged on a marked plane closer than the intended plane; `far` means staged beyond it. Choose both sufficiently separated to give visibly defocused intended ROI under the frozen aperture. `near-focused` means staged close to the reference optimum, without using the timed attempt's outcome to choose it. In the authorized session, use an independently repeatable ordinary manual-focus recipe if available: focus on the fixed staging target, lock/retain focus through the supported mode transition, return to the intended composition, and verify the start crop. If this transition refocuses or cannot be repeated, stop and revise/freeze the recipe before acquisition. Do not assume focus-ring increments are calibrated positions. No endpoint forcing or service control is allowed by this protocol.

Photograph staging geometry and write a per-combination checklist. Restage before every attempt, including repeats after failures. Record direction and available start-position evidence; absent lens coordinates remain null. A changed fixture is a new revision, with earlier attempts retained. Pause on illumination drift, target/camera movement, or an unrepeatable start.

## Timing with manual photography and video

Record the operator trigger and camera confirmation in the same external video where possible. The visible onset of half-press is the reproducible request proxy, **not measured AF-request acceptance inside the camera**. Annotate a bracket between the last frame without the event and the first with it; use presentation timestamps, not nominal frame count alone. Separately bracket the first visible confirmation indication. Report the difference as confirmation latency from the declared proxy.

If the start bracket is [s0,s1] and confirmation bracket is [c0,c1], its latency interval is [max(0,c0−s1), c1−s0]. Add measured clock/synchronization or visibility uncertainty conservatively; ambiguous/occluded events produce wider bounds or missing timing. Measure actual frame spacing and note duplicate/dropped frames. If using multiple recordings, record synchronization and its uncertainty; without synchronization do not subtract their timestamps. Milliseconds are storage units, not millisecond accuracy. Preserve original clips and annotations for independent re-reading.

Before trials, validate an ordinary capture recipe that preserves the focus state at confirmation or deadline and does not initiate another AF cycle. Capture promptly and log the confirmation-to-capture delay and shutter event. If release/capture continues or restarts focusing, final-image quality cannot establish quality at the timed event; classify that relationship as unobserved and revise the recipe. A later sharp image cannot turn a deadline failure into an on-time success. Capture a still at the defined confirmation or deadline procedure and evaluate its intended-plane quality independently. A saved still verifies quality at capture and cannot timestamp first acceptable focus. The default result is therefore **confirmation latency plus independently verified final quality**, with `acquisition_latency_ms = null`. A camera display or AF confirmation alone cannot supply independent acceptable-focus onset. Continuous ROI observations can provide an onset interval only after their quality domain, threshold and clock are independently validated; saved-image thresholds do not transfer to display video automatically. Frame rate is not AF-statistics cadence. Request acceptance, sensor delivery, lens commands, movement completion, and search iterations are unobserved here; record search-cost/runtime fields as unavailable, not zero.

## Independent quality and reference sweep

Specify the intended plane and fixed ROI in image coordinates before trials; include a visible target-plane marker or geometry record. Place an edge/texture target on that plane for a reproducible edge-acutance measure in fixed saved-image processing. Avoid saturation, motion blur, sharpening changes, and exposure shifts masquerading as focus improvement. P04 still needs an evaluable independent target; if its target cannot be measured without changing the AF challenge, document the independent measurement region and its relationship to the intended plane.

Before timed acquisitions, obtain an ordinary bounded manual-focus reference sweep with fixed geometry/settings, bracketing the sharpest intended-plane image from both directions. Use a finite maximum of 15 focus positions and three best-position repeat captures for initial calibration. No physical range or step size is assumed. If the best sample is at a sweep edge, the metric is unstable, or best-position repeats cannot support a threshold, stop and revise the bounded calibration rather than extending indefinitely. Record position order, direction, capture times, processing and actual settings; a manual stack gives no precise motor timing.

Normalize intended-ROI edge acutance by the same-scene sweep maximum. Independently inspect plane correctness: a sharper background does not rescue an unsharp intended subject. Use the best-position repeat spread and an agreed visibly acceptable intended-plane range to set a quality threshold and repeatability bound **before timed outcomes are inspected**. These values remain null until calibration, and their frozen values/method live in the session snapshot. Require both intended-plane correctness and quality above threshold for success. If objective quality is ambiguous, classify incomplete with uncertainty; do not substitute the stock contrast score. Randomize anonymized final-image review where feasible and retain disagreement notes. Recheck reference quality after the block/session to expose drift; invalidate affected acquisition records under the same documented rule, without discarding them.

## Attempts, deadlines, failures and fallback

The administrative acquisition deadline is 10 seconds from the request proxy, applied to every attempt. This is a finite collection bound, not an estimated X-M1 performance target. A complete attempt window is at most 15 seconds: acquisition plus up to 5 seconds for the prescribed final capture/annotation. Staging and reference calibration are separate, logged preparatory actions with their own bounds. If manual stop/capture overshoots, record actual timestamps and protocol error; no result after 10 seconds counts as an on-time acquisition. Stop the authorized session on abnormal state, uncontrolled motion, repeated malfunction, or inability to restore/verify stock operation, and use the reviewed incident path.

Use existing benchmark-trial v1 records for every initiated attempt, including interruption, bad video, invalid staging and replacements. Outcome meanings are: success (on-time completion and independent acceptable intended-plane quality); timeout (no qualifying completion by deadline); wrong-focus (wrong plane or below-threshold focus without a false confirmation); false-confirmation (camera reports completion but independent quality/plane fails); incomplete (quality/completion cannot be established); cancelled (operator interruption); protocol-error (recipe/recording/configuration violation). Preserve secondary flags so a timeout with a wrong-plane final image remains discoverable. An interval spanning the deadline cannot establish on-time success; retain censoring/ambiguity and count conservatively as non-success.

Stock-only trials have `fallback_used = false`. A future candidate's stock fallback must use the original request origin and same deadline, include its entire time, and report frequency/reason separately. No restart of the stopwatch or dropping fallback attempts is allowed. Cancellation and protocol-error attempts stay in the conservative all-attempt analysis with deadline penalty; also report their counts and a frozen valid-only sensitivity analysis. Replacement attempts have new IDs, explicit reasons and links to originals; replacements never overwrite records.

Where independent onset is observed, report successes' latency plus a mean capped at the 10-second deadline, assigning the deadline to failed/invalid attempts. Where onset is unavailable, report failure fraction and the corresponding capped **confirmation proxy** metric, labelled as such; do not fill missing acquisition with confirmation. Unobserved onset is missing evidence; deadline-censored onset intervals remain intervals. Show missingness and the pessimistic deadline-penalty sensitivity. Exclusion rules and weights must be identical for future stock/candidate comparisons.

## Order, pairing and storage

The manifest fixes seed `E01-stock-pilot-v1`. For repeat blocks 01–10, sort combinations by ascending SHA-256 hex digest of ASCII `seed|two-digit-repeat|combination_id`, breaking any tie by combination ID. Visit every combination once per block. Save the complete order before collection and record actual order/deviations. This deterministic permutation requires no language-specific random generator.

Use pair ID `E01:<scene_id>:<start>:<two-digit-repeat>`. Stock-only pair IDs reserve matching slots; they do not imply a candidate exists. For future comparisons collect contemporaneous stock/candidate pairs within matched fixture/start/lens/light/aperture, with controller order sorted by SHA-256 of ASCII `seed|pair_id|controller` (tie by controller). Restage both and record drift; this old pilot is not a substitute for contemporaneous stock controls. Record cancellations before the trigger as schedule deviations; after trigger they are attempts.

Every trial binds a settings artifact by path/hash. Bundle protocol snapshot, seed/order, attempt/pair/start/scene/session IDs, actual settings and builds, event brackets/clocks, quality/plane assessment, reference sweep, fallback/exclusion reasons and all raw artifact hashes. Existing trial v1 cannot inline seed, start, intervals or grouping metadata: bind a private settings JSON carrying protocol artifact hash, seed, combination/start/session, and point to a hashed annotation JSON in `raw_artifacts`. The annotation stores event intervals, validity, secondary outcome flags, reference/ROI and reviewer notes. No extended trial schema or invented synthetic observation is necessary for this design deliverable. Use `hardware-observed` only for actual future measurements; this protocol is `static-inference` and is not a trial.

## Splits, analysis and decisions

P01–P05 and pilot sessions are development data. Reserve H01–H05 as wholly new scene fixtures, covering the same five categories, and CONFIRM-01/02 as separate future acquisition sessions. These are reserved names, not observed sessions. Keep their entire acquisitions/reference sweeps out of model fitting, fixture/threshold selection, and candidate tuning. Never split neighboring frames from a sweep across training and confirmation. If held-out data influence a revision, retire that holdout and acquire new held-out scenes/sessions.

Report each combination and scenario separately, with equal scene weights and equal start weights within scene; P01's extra start must not silently increase bright-scene weight. Show counts, failures by cause, missingness, fallback, intended-plane quality distribution, median, and descriptive p95. Ten repeats per combination do not substantiate a precise p95. Report timing intervals and observer uncertainty with statistics. Use scene/session as correlation units; 110 repetitions are not 110 independent scenes. Pilot uncertainty can be descriptive when too few clusters exist for meaningful intervals.

Use this stock pilot to repair measurements, estimate variance and plan confirmatory sample size. Before held-out collection freeze candidate/build, scene list/weights, settings/start recipes, success and quality definitions, deadline/fallback/exclusions, sample size/stopping rule, seed/order and analysis. Freeze a 95% hierarchical paired resampling procedure preserving scene/session clusters and pairs, plus interval sensitivity for timing. Choose sufficient independent scenes/sessions from pilot variance; two reserved session names alone do not prove adequate power. If timing precision or clusters cannot support the desired comparison, narrow the claim or declare inconclusive.

The specification's provisional choices are 20% lower median acquisition latency, at most 10% worse p95 in adequately sampled required scenarios, at most two percentage points lower success, and no material quality loss. They are acceptance choices, not predicted gains. Set the quality noninferiority margin from independent repeatability and record any target revision before confirmation. Confidence bounds, not point estimates, support pass/fail; intervals crossing a required boundary or inadequate p95/success-margin evidence mean inconclusive. If acceptable-focus onset remains unmeasured, confirmation speed plus quality cannot prove the acquisition-latency target. This pilot supplies no acceptance decision. Future G6 also needs the full cancellation/invalid-observation/unsupported-mode/removal and restoration regressions in the specification.

## Offline validation and handoff

The checked-in devenv host environment (Python 3.14.7; check-jsonschema 0.38.0) accepted the draft-2020-12 metaschema and this manifest. Nine mutations were rejected: unknown version, frozen status, camera authorization, dropping attempts, zero failure penalty, treating confirmation as acceptable-focus onset, negative quality threshold, a missing setting and a missing combination. Independent semantic checks verified unique IDs, scene/start/scenario consistency, 110 planned slots, null settings, disjoint held-out scenes, matched finite deadlines and complete deterministic repeat blocks. The schedule hash for ASCII `two-digit-repeat:combination_id` lines including trailing newlines is `05d509d2c1c8ff12e6b312b90e1895a6708aeb080f52fc1ac9c4af10e4470f9b`.

Integrated validation passed with `devenv shell -- bazel test --lockfile_mode=error --repository_disable_download //tests:research-contracts`; it covers manifest/schema validation and substantive policy/cross-field rejection cases. Host validation neither resolves private session requirements nor measures autofocus. The 1.50 collection is deferred until AF work begins.
