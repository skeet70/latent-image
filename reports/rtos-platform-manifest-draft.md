# RTOS platform replacement: interface inventory and project plan

## Evidence and decision boundary

This is the first RTOS-platform research deliverable for #117/#127, incorporating #142, #143, #150, #151 and #152. It inventories X-M1 firmware 1.50 interfaces and missing contracts. It does not qualify a kernel replacement. The proposed milestones below are **[INFERENCE]**. Existing source and runtime claims retain their original scope; the generation command performs host-only source analysis. The replacement process permits new internal function boundaries and requires compatibility at retained interfaces, including shared objects, startup and hardware protocols. [Sources: `spec/11-incremental-firmware-replacement.md:11-19,33-58,79-99`; `reports/replacement-inventory-baseline.md` §§8.8-8.17.]

The current machine-readable artifact is `private-data/scratch/RtosCensus/manifest-v2.json` (E-RTOS-SLOT-MANIFEST-V2-150); the first generation `private-data/scratch/RtosManifest/manifest.json` (E-RTOS-PLATFORM-MANIFEST-150) is preserved. `schemas/rtos-manifest.schema.json` describes both. The generator is `replacement/media_permit/RtosManifestMain.hs`, target `//replacement/media_permit:rtos-manifest`; its versioned non-root/law inventory is `replacement/media_permit/rtos_surface.json` and its reviewed function-boundary input is `replacement/media_permit/rtos_function_boundaries.json`. A slot's semantic recovery and extent recovery are separate fields. `recovered` means arguments, result, every error path, context and touched objects are closed from both domains' instructions. `partial` means the cited contract has at least one material gap. `unknown` means no semantic contract is established here. `analogue` names a µITRON 4.0 service only as a hypothesis the X-M1 body was checked against; it never fills a field. Empty error/object/caller lists mean no established entries in this inventory, not absence. `null` argument/result/context means unrecovered. No slot is promoted to `recovered` solely because its target or extent is known. [Source: generator `contract`, `extent` and `main`; schema.]

## Root surface: 97 callable slots in each domain

The independent publishers contain 93 callable pointers at offsets `0..0x170` and four extensions at `0x1618/0x161c/0x1620/0x1624`. Each root also contains a tick pointer at `+0x174`, 120 eleven-word descriptors from `+0x178` and a stack-top word at `+0x1628`. The complete root is 1,419 words, `0x162c` bytes. Root A is `0x4004dae0`; root B is `0x400ddaa0`; publication cells are `0x96bd20/0x96bd24`. These are 97 callable slots per root, 194 slot instances, not 1,419 services. The installer publishes roots and bookkeeping rather than constructing a scheduler; B root publication precedes its late initialization. [Sources: `replacement/primary_initializer/src.rs:18-50,71-100`; `replacement/interface_initializer/src.rs:19-49,57-85`; Inventory §8.12; E-KERNEL-PHASE0-INSTALLATION-SOURCE-150, E-KERNEL-PHASE0-RESOURCE-ORDER-SOURCE-150.]

The generated manifest enumerates every offset and both targets from those publishers. It attempts every target's extent through the reviewed `StockInventory.deriveFunction`. The candidate upper fence is the nearest later published entry, direct A32 BL target, A-domain Ghidra export, reviewed function boundary bound to the same domain's loader digest, or image end; B never borrows A exports, A boundaries or a relocation delta. The derivation admits no external-tail declarations. A successful record includes the derived reachable code envelope, classified code/literal window and return-proof premises. A refusal includes its diagnostic and claims no function end. These envelopes are not whole-kernel byte totals or transitive helper closure. Next-entry subtraction alone is never reported as a function size. [Sources: `replacement/media_permit/StockInventory.hs`; `replacement/media_permit/ReturnProvenance.hs`; generator `extent` and `makeDomain`.]

Four independently reviewed tool changes extend what the derivation can prove without weakening any refusal. `LSL` by a constant and register shifts with a known amount are multiplications that keep an index while the frame is private (commit `1a111037`). MRS yields the current status word; MSR to the CPSR control or extension field is admitted only from a value whose mode, T and E bits provably equal the current ones, flags-only writes from any source, and every SPSR, immediate, banked or mode-changing form refuses (`24164be2`). SXTB/SXTH carry their source's provenance. A new premise `loaded-object-frame-isolation`, recorded at each store that relies on it, assumes that a word loaded through an address not derived from the frame, while the frame is unexposed, does not address its unexposed words; a load that may read a declared saved stack pointer (both kernels' T tables, 120 records of 56 bytes with the saved SP at +0x18, written by the dispatchers at A `0x4364c/0x43794`, B `0xd364c/0xd3794`) yields unknown, relying on loaded pointers addressing a record start and indexed globals lying in their array or at most one record before it (`b8862355`). The remaining saved-SP route is a saved SP held as an unknown value and used as the index from a constant base, admitted under `global-object-stack-separation` like any unknown index. In the per-slot review no premise-backed store in a slot window goes through a value loaded from a saved-SP field; the SP-saving stores lie only in the dispatchers, which slots reach as calls. Derivations in the create/delete cluster under `global-object-stack-separation` rely on a caller precondition: unbounded `uxth(id)-1` indices stay inside their tables only for valid ids. [Sources: `replacement/media_permit/ReturnProvenance.hs` premise comments; `emulation/README.md` stock-inventory premises; reviews RtosCensus.ExtentReview and RtosCensus.SysRegReview.]

Every slot carries argument/result/context fields, known errors, known touched objects, direct-image4 BL matches, source-evidenced computed wrapper entries, evidence and an open caller-closure statement. The linear BL scan includes conditional BL encodings but cannot distinguish embedded data from reachable instructions; it is a candidate census. Named computed wrappers are lower bounds, not all computed uses. Tail calls, Thumb, root copies, computed addresses, aliases and later executable writers remain obligations. No complete all-image caller/dataflow analysis is established by this deliverable. [Sources: generator `instructions`/`main`; Inventory §§8.11,8.13,8.16; `spec/11-incremental-firmware-replacement.md:43,53,91`.]

The first reviewed host invocation (E-RTOS-PLATFORM-MANIFEST-150) emitted all194 slot instances with27 partial and70 unknown contracts per domain and derived23 extents, refusing74:68 at unadmitted status-register instructions, five at incoming-return proofs and one at an unclassified window. The second invocation (E-RTOS-SLOT-MANIFEST-V2-150) records per domain24 recovered,30 partial and43 unknown contracts, and74 derived and23 refused extents; no previously derived slot is lost. The74 derived envelopes sum to19,416 A bytes and19,420 B bytes, not whole-kernel size. All23 remaining refusals per domain are incoming-return proofs at the same offsets in A and B (`+0x14,+0x18,+0x1c,+0x20,+0x50,+0x54,+0x70,+0x8c,+0x94,+0x9c,+0xb0,+0xbc,+0xc0,+0xc4,+0xc8,+0xd4,+0xd8,+0xdc,+0xe0,+0xec,+0xf4,+0xf8,+0x124`): seven use a base set only on the same predicated path, six store through an incoming argument kept in a callee-saved register, seven store through a pointer reloaded from a frame word holding a spilled argument, and three join pointer classes or error constants. The direct-image4 scan finds two A `+0x16c` candidates at `0x72b00c/0x72b068` and no B candidates. The output validates against the schema. [Sources: both manifests; E-RTOS-PLATFORM-MANIFEST-150, E-RTOS-SLOT-MANIFEST-V2-150.]

### Stock slot-call census

One independently reviewed run of `//emulation:rtos-census` on the canonical stock no-card inputs covers cold reset, START MENU `(69,52,12)` at80M, the shooting menu `(1,5,2)` at382M and the #128 FILM SIMULATION trajectory through film list `(1,254,0)`, return, repeat and handback `(1,5,6)`, ending at848.75M instructions. Instruction points at all194 slot targets record1,113,846 entries and1,113,765 matched returns;81 calls are open at the end. A fetch watch over the194 entry words reconciles every entry with exactly one stopped and one resumed fetch row and finds no unstopped entry execution, so no slot target ran through an unobserved alias. Every entry code word equals the canonical loader and every published root word equals its target. [Source: `private-data/scratch/RtosCensus/census-v1/census.json`; E-RTOS-SLOT-CENSUS-STOCK-150.]

73 of194 instances are reached: A50 and B23. The busiest are A `+0x1618` core identity (306,768 entries), `+0x148` context sense (145,191), `+0x1624` critical observe (104,815), `+0x16c` current-task query (67,882) and `+0x128` current-task index (65,129, only from inside `+0x16c`). Every reached instance returns status0 except the expected refusals: `+0x78` semaphore poll returns-50 on220 of221 calls, `+0xc4` data-queue poll returns-50 once, and `+0xc` activate returns-25 at startup. A `+0x1618/+0x164/+0x168` also run on core1 and B `+0x0/+0x80/+0xa0/+0xe4` on core0, so the executing core does not identify the slot domain. Sleep and wakeup counts match task for task: every task that slept through A `+0x24` was woken by exactly one A `+0x2c`, A `+0x30` or B `+0x2c` call, including cross-domain wakeups of class-1 tasks95 and100 by B task88. Thirty reached instances (24 offsets) were manifest-unknown; all24 offsets now carry contracts. The census is an observer-reported finite route without a receipt consumer or refusal controls; unreached slots are not observed, not unreachable. [Sources: census `.reach[]`; `private-data/scratch/RtosCensus/census-v1-unknown-entries.json`; `private-data/scratch/RtosCensus/contracts/*.json`.]

### µITRON 4.0 hypothesis against X-M1 bodies

The recovered bodies match µITRON 4.0 numbering and constants where they overlap (E_SYS-5, E_PAR-17, E_ID-18, E_CTX-25, E_OBJ-41, E_NOEXS-42, E_QOVR-43, E_RLWAI-49, E_TMOUT-50), and where recovered the order `0x64..0x154` follows the specification's chapter order for semaphores, eventflags, data queues, cyclic handlers, system state and dispatch control. Ready-queue rotation sits at `+0x11c`, one slot earlier than a strict chapter-order prediction (`+0x120`); the task-context current-task index is `+0x128` and its non-task variant `+0x12c`. The X-M1 kernel departs from the specification in ways a replacement must preserve: `+0x68/+0x84` deletes check nothing, ignore the class bits, return0 for any id and neither release waiters with E_DLT nor return-42; `+0x4` task delete takes a mode argument and no checks; `+0x108/+0x10c` cyclic create/delete skip id and context checks; `+0x158/+0x15c` report-5 rather than E_PAR; the context word is an IRQ nesting counter that the dispatcher idle loop also sets; dispatch-disabled is a separate word (A `0x40054764`, B `0x400e4724`) read by `+0x24`, `+0x140..+0x154` and the IRQ-exit dispatcher. [Sources: contract files; `private-data/analysis/x-m1-1.50/decode/loader-40000.dis` and `loader-d0000.dis` cited per slot in the manifest; audit RtosCensus.ContractAudit.]

### Proposed issue text

The inventory has97 callable slots per root; it also identifies retained C/T descriptor and tick-pointer consumers, so replacing root targets alone cannot retire the platform.
