# RTOS platform replacement: interface inventory and project plan

## Evidence and decision boundary

This is the first RTOS-platform research deliverable for #117/#127, incorporating #142, #143, #150, #151 and #152. It inventories X-M1 firmware 1.50 interfaces and missing contracts. It does not qualify a kernel replacement. The proposed milestones below are **[INFERENCE]**. Existing source and runtime claims retain their original scope; the generation command performs host-only source analysis. The replacement process permits new internal function boundaries and requires compatibility at retained interfaces, including shared objects, startup and hardware protocols. [Sources: `spec/11-incremental-firmware-replacement.md:11-19,33-58,79-99`; `reports/replacement-inventory-baseline.md` §§8.8-8.17.]

The current machine-readable artifact is `private-data/scratch/RtosDecomp/manifest-v6.json` (E-RTOS-SLOT-MANIFEST-V5-150). It re-derives every slot contract from the kernel C of the x70view decomp at `55436fc85c0560dd252b0410af6f2427fe198cd3`: `decomp/firmwares/xm1-150/kernel0` is loader A and `kernel1` is loader B ([v6 contracts from the decomp](#v6-contracts-from-the-decomp)).

The earlier generations are preserved:
- `private-data/scratch/RtosContracts/manifest-v5.json` and `manifest-v4.json`;
- `private-data/scratch/RtosCensus/manifest-v3.json` and `manifest-v2.json`;
- `private-data/scratch/RtosManifest/manifest.json`.

`schemas/rtos-manifest.schema.json` describes v4, v5 and v6. Each generation added fields:
- v3: per-slot `preconditions` and the top-level `savedStackTaintResiduals`;
- v4: per-slot `experiments` (proposed census arms and native cases for facts the source cannot settle), `gaps` and `domainNotes`;
- v6: per-slot `decomp` sources, the top-level `decompCommit` and the extent status `decomp-exact`.

The generator is `replacement/media_permit/RtosManifestMain.hs`, target `//replacement/media_permit:rtos-manifest`. Its versioned inputs are:
- the non-root/law inventory `replacement/media_permit/rtos_surface.json`;
- the reviewed function-boundary input `replacement/media_permit/rtos_function_boundaries.json`;
- the reviewed slot-contract input `replacement/media_permit/rtos_slot_contracts.json`, which must hold exactly one contract per published offset; unknown keys and repeated or unpublished offsets are refused;
- the reviewed decomp-extent input `replacement/media_permit/rtos_decomp_extents.json`.

A slot's semantic recovery and extent recovery are separate fields:
- `recovered` means arguments, result, every error path, context and touched objects are closed from both domains' code.
- `partial` means the cited contract has at least one material gap.
- `analogue` names a µITRON 4.0 service only as a hypothesis the X-M1 body was checked against; it never fills a field.
- A `preconditions` entry names a caller obligation the body does not check.
- Empty error/object/caller lists mean no established entries in this inventory, not absence.
- A `decomp` source with `exact: false` is a NONMATCHING draft or a declaration without a body. Every fact taken from one is confirmed against the stock disassembly, and the contract cites that disassembly.

## Root surface: 97 callable slots in each domain

The independent publishers contain 93 callable pointers at offsets `0..0x170` and four extensions at `0x1618/0x161c/0x1620/0x1624`. Each root also contains a tick pointer at `+0x174`, 120 eleven-word descriptors from `+0x178` and a stack-top word at `+0x1628`. The complete root is 1,419 words, `0x162c` bytes. Root A is `0x4004dae0`; root B is `0x400ddaa0`; publication cells are `0x96bd20/0x96bd24`. These are 97 callable slots per root, 194 slot instances, not 1,419 services. The installer publishes roots and bookkeeping rather than constructing a scheduler; B root publication precedes its late initialization. [Sources: `replacement/primary_initializer/src.rs:18-50,71-100`; `replacement/interface_initializer/src.rs:19-49,57-85`; Inventory §8.12; E-KERNEL-PHASE0-INSTALLATION-SOURCE-150, E-KERNEL-PHASE0-RESOURCE-ORDER-SOURCE-150.]

The generated manifest enumerates every offset and both targets from those publishers. It attempts every target's extent through the reviewed `StockInventory.deriveFunction`. The candidate upper fence is the nearest later published entry, direct A32 BL target, A-domain Ghidra export, reviewed function boundary bound to the same domain's loader digest, or image end; B never borrows A exports, A boundaries or a relocation delta. The derivation admits no external-tail declarations. A successful record includes the derived reachable code envelope, classified code/literal window and return-proof premises. A refusal includes its diagnostic and claims no function end. These envelopes are not whole-kernel byte totals or transitive helper closure. Next-entry subtraction alone is never reported as a function size. [Sources: `replacement/media_permit/StockInventory.hs`; `replacement/media_permit/ReturnProvenance.hs`; generator `extent` and `makeDomain`.]

Five independently reviewed tool changes extend or bound what the derivation can prove without weakening any refusal. `LSL` by a constant and register shifts with a known amount are multiplications that keep an index while the frame is private (commit `1a111037`). MRS yields the current status word; MSR to the CPSR control or extension field is admitted only from a value whose mode, T and E bits provably equal the current ones, flags-only writes from any source, and every SPSR, immediate, banked or mode-changing form refuses (`24164be2`). SXTB/SXTH carry their source's provenance. A new premise `loaded-object-frame-isolation`, recorded at each store that relies on it, assumes that a word loaded through an address not derived from the frame, while the frame is unexposed, does not address its unexposed words (`b8862355`). Both kernels' T tables are declared as saved-stack arrays (120 records of 56 bytes with the saved SP at +0x18, written by the dispatchers at A `0x4364c/0x43794`, B `0xd364c/0xd3794`). A word load through a tracked address that may read such a field yields a saved-stack value whose derivatives no premise accepts: indices built from it are not global objects, and storing through it, storing it outside the frame, passing it to a call or external tail or returning it refuses. This relies on loaded pointers addressing a record start and indexed globals lying in their array or at most one record before it. Six open residuals remain, ways a saved SP can escape the taint for which the derivation claims nothing; the manifest lists each in `savedStackTaintResiduals` with its statement: `sub-word-load`, `sub-word-or-unaligned-store`, `untracked-pointer`, `joined-address`, `unknown-first-operand-address` and `call-boundary`. The maintainer keeps them as documented open gaps for now. The exclusion leaves every RTOS slot extent and the media-permit stock inventory unchanged; in the controller manifest it moves 0xc6e798, 0xc6ee04 and 0xc7b1f0 from conditional to unresolved. That is a model limitation, not a fault in those functions: BFI is modelled as an opaque compute, so the following table index is frame-derived, its load is tainted and a dead tainted r3 at the next BL refuses. In the per-slot review no premise-backed store in a slot window goes through a value loaded from a saved-SP field; the SP-saving stores lie only in the dispatchers, which slots reach as calls. Fourteen create/delete-cluster slots carry the named caller precondition `valid-object-id`: their unbounded `uxth(id)-1` indices stay inside their tables only for valid ids. [Sources: `replacement/media_permit/ReturnProvenance.hs` premise comments and `SavedStackTaintResidual`; `emulation/README.md` stock-inventory premises; manifest `preconditions` and `savedStackTaintResiduals`; reviews RtosCensus.ExtentReview, RtosCensus.SysRegReview and RtosCensus.TaintReview.]

Every slot carries argument/result/context fields, known errors, known touched objects, direct-image4 BL matches, source-evidenced computed wrapper entries, evidence and an open caller-closure statement. The linear BL scan includes conditional BL encodings but cannot distinguish embedded data from reachable instructions; it is a candidate census. Named computed wrappers are lower bounds, not all computed uses. Tail calls, Thumb, root copies, computed addresses, aliases and later executable writers remain obligations. No complete all-image caller/dataflow analysis is established by this deliverable. [Sources: generator `instructions`/`main`; Inventory §§8.11,8.13,8.16; `spec/11-incremental-firmware-replacement.md:43,53,91`.]

### Stock slot-call census (observer-reported discovery census)

The census ranks slot reach and directs contract research. It is observer-reported discovery evidence, not qualification evidence: it has no receipt consumer or refusal controls, and a qualification that cites it needs both first. [Owner decision recorded in E-RTOS-SLOT-CENSUS-STOCK-150.]

A fetch watch over the194 entry words reconciles every entry with exactly one stopped and one resumed fetch row and finds no unstopped entry execution, so no slot target ran through an unobserved alias. Every entry code word equals the canonical loader and every published root word equals its target. [Source: `private-data/scratch/RtosCensus/census-v1/census.json`; E-RTOS-SLOT-CENSUS-STOCK-150.]

### µITRON 4.0 hypothesis against X-M1 bodies

The recovered bodies match µITRON 4.0 numbering and constants where they overlap (E_SYS-5, E_PAR-17, E_ID-18, E_CTX-25, E_ILUSE-28, E_OBJ-41, E_NOEXS-42, E_QOVR-43, E_RLWAI-49, E_TMOUT-50), and the slots `0x0..0x114` follow the specification's chapter order for task management, task-dependent synchronization, task exceptions, semaphores, eventflags, data queues, mailboxes, fixed-size pools, system time and cyclic handlers. System state departs from it: `+0x118..+0x124` are four ready-queue rotations (task or handler context, r0-selected or executing domain) where the specification has `rot_rdq/irot_rdq`, followed by the current-task index `+0x128` and its non-task variant `+0x12c`, CPU lock/unlock `+0x130..+0x13c`, dispatch control and sense. `+0x170` is an unchecked pool get outside the chapter order. The X-M1 kernel departs from the specification in ways a replacement must preserve: the deletes `+0x68/+0x84/+0xa4/+0xd0/+0xe8` check nothing, ignore the class bits, return0 for any id and neither release waiters with E_DLT nor return-42; `+0x4` task delete takes a mode argument and no checks; `+0x108/+0x10c` cyclic create/delete and the creates `+0xa0/+0xcc/+0xe4` skip id and context checks; `+0xa8` data-queue send and `+0x170` pool get check no context at all; `+0xbc` accepts only class-0 ids; `+0x118` returns0 for every rejection; eventflag waits `+0x94/+0x98` refuse a second waiter with-28; suspension `+0x40` does not nest (a second suspend returns-43) and `+0x48` forced resume is a call-through to `+0x44`; system time `+0xfc/+0x100` is one 32-bit word; `+0x158/+0x15c` report-5 rather than E_PAR; the context word is an IRQ nesting counter that the dispatcher idle loop also sets; dispatch-disabled is a separate word (A `0x40054764`, B `0x400e4724`) read by `+0x24`, `+0x140..+0x154` and the IRQ-exit dispatcher. The timed waits `+0x28/+0xb4/+0xc8/+0xe0/+0xf4` arm their deadline on the executing domain's next-clock word (A `0x40054770`, B `0x400e4730`, advanced by10 per tick) even when the object belongs to the other class. [Sources: contract files; `replacement/media_permit/rtos_slot_contracts.json`; `private-data/analysis/x-m1-1.50/decode/loader-40000.dis` and `loader-d0000.dis` cited per slot in the manifest; audit RtosCensus.ContractAudit.]

### v6 contracts from the decomp

**Exactness.** Kernel0 and kernel1 are the same C compiled with a domain constant. At `55436fc85c` each carries 19 NONMATCHING markers. A fact from a NONMATCHING function, or from a body the decomp only declares, is in the manifest only after it was checked against the stock disassembly of both loaders.

**Contracts.** All 97 contracts per domain are `recovered`, and 48 offsets carry caller preconditions.
- Against v5: 19 confirm, 33 complete and 45 correct (`private-data/scratch/RtosDecomp/{task,semflag,queue,pooltime,system}/contracts.json`, field `comparison`).
- The extent derivation of v5 is unchanged: 80 StockInventory envelopes per domain. Each envelope's size equals the size of the decomp function at the same address wherever one exists (136 of 160 instances).
- Of v5's 17 refused extents per domain, 15 are now `decomp-exact`: the byte range of a NONMATCHING-free decomp function at the target, hashed against the loader. StockInventory's refusal premises stay recorded and unproved.
- `+0xc0` and `+0xd4` remain refused, because their decomp bodies are NONMATCHING.

**Corrections with design consequences:**
- **Lock acquire never fails.** Every class record's scheduler-lock and object-lock acquire (`+0xc`, `+0x18`) is A `0x439d8` / B `0xd398c`. It spins with ldrex/strex until it holds the word and always returns 0. Release A `0x43a14` / B `0xd39c8` is DMB, store 0, DSB. v5's "failed try, unmask IRQ, retry" paths in every lock-taking service are unreachable; the only live restart is the running task's ready-bit check in blocking services.
- **Two lock words per domain.** The scheduler lock is A `0x400545c0` / B `0x400e4580` and the object lock A `0x400545c4` / B `0x400e4584`. Record `+0x0` is a dispatch target (0 in the own record, 1 or 2 in the other), not a core selector.
- **Global lock `0x400c0804`.** `+0x161c/+0x1620/+0x1624` are a single try that returns -1 when the word is held, an unconditional release and an observe. Both cores' IRQ vectors hold the same word for the whole handler (tags 0x11/0x12), and the image4 critical pair holds it with 0x21/0x22. From a handler `+0x161c` always returns -1, and a task-context holder must keep IRQ masked until release.
- **Eventflags are general.** `+0x80/+0x84` create and delete over 120 flag records without class bits. Only task create `0x725dd8` makes them per task, by passing the task's own id. An eventflag has at most one waiter; a second gets -28. The interrupt-context set `+0x8c` readies a satisfied waiter through the flag's domain, not the waiter's (A `0x48544`, B `0xd8504`; precondition `same-domain-waiter`).
- **Units.** Timeouts and delays (`+0x28`, `+0x4c`, `+0x7c`, `+0x9c`, `+0xb4`, `+0xc8`, `+0xe0`, `+0xf4`) are in kernel clock units, deadline = domain clock word + value, and the clock advances by 10 per tick.
- **Tick `+0x104`.** It does not hold its lock across user code: `cyclic_expire` releases it around the handler, and the tick unmasks IRQ after every callback. The whole tick runs inside this core's IRQ vector holding `0x400c0804`.
- **Cyclic hazards.** Deleting an active handler (`+0x10c`) or a zero period makes the tick loop without returning, and calls handler 0 (preconditions `stopped-before-delete`, `nonzero-period`).
- **Other changes:**
  - `+0x16c` takes no argument and returns the running task's id, or 0 in handler context or with IRQ masked.
  - `+0x18` can return with IRQ still masked when both cores terminate the same waiting task.
  - `+0x1c` reorders only waiters in state 0x32 on priority-ordered queues.
  - `+0x158/+0x15c` accept negative interrupt numbers and index below their tables (precondition `valid-irq-number`).
  - Passing task number 0 with a foreign class acts on the caller's own task under the other domain's lock (`self-selector-class`, seven slots).

### Proposed issue text

Move #127's RTOS retirement work into one platform project behind the two domain roots plus direct scheduler/vector/device interfaces. The inventory has 97 callable slots per root, and it identifies retained C/T descriptor and tick-pointer consumers, so replacing root targets alone cannot retire the platform.

The x70view decomp of the two loader kernels is byte-exact apart from 19 marked functions each. Reading it closed every slot contract: per domain 97 are recovered, and 95 of 97 extents are derived or hashed against byte-exact functions. It also gave typed layouts for the task, context, wait, timer and object records.

The code settles the cross-core design:
- the two kernels form one scheduler with two run queues and edit each other's ready queues, timer heaps and object queues directly;
- lock acquire never fails;
- a global lock serializes both cores' interrupt handlers and the image4 critical sections.

The plan is to own the 17 CPU-local slots first, then both domains' schedulers together behind five slots that stay stock-exact (task create/delete, global lock). Owned code must keep one ready, never-blocking task per domain while the stock vector runs. The remaining gates are hardware laws (memory attributes, cross-core exclusive monitors, line 32 on core 1), the two NONMATCHING data-queue and mailbox bodies, and runtime confirmation of source-only contracts. Link this report, [RTOS platform options](rtos-platform-options.md) §4.13 and ledger records E-RTOS-SLOT-CENSUS-STOCK-150, E-RTOS-SLOT-MANIFEST-V5-150 and E-IMAGE4-QUEUE-DESTRUCTOR-POWEROFF-150 after integration.
